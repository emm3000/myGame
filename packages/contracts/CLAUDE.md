# packages/contracts

The wire vocabulary `apps/web` and `apps/api` share: zod schemas and the types inferred from them.

## Commands

- `pnpm --filter @mygame/contracts test` — Vitest, `src/**/*.test.ts`.
- `pnpm --filter @mygame/contracts typecheck` — `tsc --noEmit` against `tsconfig.base.json`.

## Gotchas

- Schemas only. No exported function, no behavior: `rg -n "^export (async )?function" packages/contracts/src` stays empty.
- One entry file. `src/index.ts` is the package's only export (`exports["."]`); consumers import `@mygame/contracts`, never a path under `src/`.
- A schema is named `<wire noun>Schema` (`HealthResponseSchema`); its type is the same noun without the suffix, inferred with `z.infer` (`HealthResponse`). Never hand-write a type that duplicates a schema.
- No dependency on the domain package until a slice needs a domain type on the wire; that slice adds it.
- `zod`, `typescript` and `vitest` versions live in the root catalog; write `catalog:` here, never a version.
- One schema per file under `src/`, named after its wire noun (`FiefOverview.ts`); `src/index.ts` only re-exports. Shared primitives (instants, whole-second durations, counts) live in `src/Wire.ts` and are not exported from the entry.
- Instants are ISO 8601 strings (`z.iso.datetime()`), durations are whole seconds; no `Date` crosses the wire.
- Wire identifiers are camelCase (`ironMine`); the Postgres enum spells it `iron_mine`, so the api adapter maps between them.
- `BuildingContentSchema` is a discriminated union by `building`; the `library` and `barracks` variants have levels without `effect`, every other variant carries the effect its building produces.
- `StartStudyRequestSchema` is `{ art }`. `FiefOverview.study` mirrors `slot` with `art` in place of `building`; `FiefOverview.arts` is a record over `ArtKind` of `{ level, resource, ratePercent, nextLevel }`, `resource` the `ResourceKind` the art raises, `nextLevel` null at the top.
- `FiefOverview.season` is `null` before the calendar's epoch, otherwise a strict `{ kind, year, endsAt, multiplierPercent, durationPercent }`: `kind` a `SeasonKind`, `year` a whole number from 1, `endsAt` the ISO instant the season ends (excluded), `multiplierPercent` a record over the five `ResourceKind`s of the whole percent from 1 the season applies to each rate, `durationPercent` a strict `{ build, study, train, road }` of the whole percents from 1 the season applies to build, study and unit training durations and to the road of a march (`road`, which the dispatch of a march or an attack reads, ADR 022). `SeasonDurationPercent.ts` holds that `{ build, study, train, road }` schema, shared by `FiefOverview.season` and `FiefContent.seasons.durationPercent` and not exported from the entry.
- `CancelStudyRequestSchema` is the path `{ art, targetLevel }`, `targetLevel` a whole count from 1.
- `UnitKindSchema` is `infantry`, `cavalry` (S16), `archer` (S20, #432) and `settler` (S18, #383), in that order; every record over `UnitKind`, `FiefContent.units` included, needs every key, so content without the cavalry, the archer or the settler terms fails the parse. `FiefContent.units` takes `strength` and `carry` as whole counts from 0 (the settler's are 0) and `durationSeconds`, `peasantOccupancy`, `roadPercent` and `barracksLevel` from 1. `ApiErrorKind` includes `BarracksTooLow` and `UnitUnfitForOrder`, the forage or attack party holding a settler.
- `FiefOverview.units` is a record over `UnitKind` of whole counts at `readAt`, the open order's delivered units included. `FiefOverview.recruitOrder` is `null` or a strict `{ unit, count, delivered, perUnitSeconds, startedAt, endsAt }`: `count` a whole count from 1, `delivered` the units delivered by `readAt`, `perUnitSeconds` a whole count from 1, `endsAt` the last delivery. `FiefOverview.recruitTerms` is a record over `UnitKind` of a strict `{ cost, peasants, perUnitSeconds }` for one unit, `perUnitSeconds` already scaled by the season's `train` percent and divided by one plus the built barracks level, with one rounding. `PlaceRecruitOrderRequestSchema` is `{ unit, count }`, `count` a whole count from 1. `CancelRecruitOrderRequestSchema` is the path `{ unit, startedAt }`, `startedAt` an ISO instant as `FiefOverview.recruitOrder.startedAt` answers it, which the path carries URL-encoded. `ApiErrorKind` includes `BarracksNotBuilt`, `RecruitSlotBusy` and `RecruitOrderNotFound`.
- `FiefOverview.march` is `null` or a strict `{ order, province, plot, terrain, units, stayHours, departedAt, oneWaySeconds, loot, arrivesAt, leavesAt, returnsAt, recalledAt, camp, fought }` (the next line gives `order`, `stayHours`, `camp` and `fought`): `province`, `plot` and `oneWaySeconds` whole counts from 1, `units` a party (below), `terrain` the target's `Terrain`, `loot` a `ResourceAmounts` fixed at dispatch and lowered only by a recall, the four instants ISO, `recalledAt` an ISO instant or `null` when the march was not recalled; no phase field, the web picks the phase from the instants. `FiefOverview.forageTerms` is a strict `{ secondsPerProvince, secondsPerPlot, maxStayHours, yieldPerHour }` as content gives it; `ForageTerms.ts` holds that schema, shared by `FiefOverview.forageTerms` and `FiefContent.forage` and not exported from the entry. `DispatchMarchRequestSchema` is `{ province, plot, units, stayHours }`, `province`, `plot` and `stayHours` whole counts from 1 and `units` a party. A party (`Party.ts`, `PartySchema`) is a record over `UnitKind` of whole counts from 0 (`UnitCounts.ts`, `UnitCountsSchema`, which `FiefOverview.units` and `battleFought.unitsLost` also use) whose counts sum at least 1; a record over an enum needs every key and refuses any other, so a party without `cavalry` fails the parse. Neither file is exported from the entry. `ApiErrorKind` includes `PlotHeld`, `MarchToOwnPlot`, `NotEnoughUnitsAtHome`, `MarchSlotBusy`, `StayOutOfRange` and `MarchTargetOutOfBounds`. `RecallMarchRequestSchema` is the path `{ departedAt }`, an ISO instant as `FiefOverview.march.departedAt` answers it, which the path carries URL-encoded. `ApiErrorKind` includes `MarchNotFound` and `MarchAlreadyReturning`.
- `FiefOverview.march` is a union on `order`: a forage march answers `order: 'forage'`, `stayHours` a whole count from 1, `camp: null` and `fought: false`; an attack answers `order: 'attack'`, `stayHours` 0, `camp` a strict `{ tier, strength }` (the tier `1`, `2` or `3`, the strength a `WholeCount` from 0) fixed at dispatch, which may differ from the map's preview, and `fought` true once the battle is past. `FiefOverview.combatTerms` is a strict `{ lootPerStrength, tiers }` copied from content; `FiefOverview.unitTerms` is a record over `UnitKind` of a strict `{ strength, carry, roadPercent, barracksLevel }` copied from `FiefContent.units`, `strength` and `carry` whole counts from 0 and the other two from 1, where each kind also carries its `cost`, `durationSeconds` and `peasantOccupancy`; `CampTiers.ts` holds the `tiers` schema, shared by `FiefOverview.combatTerms` and `FiefContent.camps` and not exported from the entry. `DispatchAttackRequestSchema` is `{ province, plot, units }`, `province` and `plot` whole counts from 1 and `units` a party.
- `FiefEventSchema` is a discriminated union on `kind` (`upgradeFinished`, `artLearned`, `upgradeCancelled`, `studyCancelled`, `recruitsDelivered`, `recruitsCancelled`, `marchReturned`, `battleFought`, `foundingSent`, `fiefFounded`, `transportSent`, `transportArrived`) of `z.strictObject` variants: the first four carry `building` or `art`, `level`, `occurredAt`, and `refund` only on the two cancels; `recruitsDelivered` carries `unit` (a `UnitKind`), `count` (a whole count from 1, the units one order delivered) and `occurredAt` (the last delivery), with no `level` and no `refund`; `recruitsCancelled` carries `unit`, `delivered` (a whole count from 0, the units the order delivered by the cancel), `cancelled` (a whole count from 1, the units it never delivered), `occurredAt` (the cancel instant) and `refund`, with no `level` and no `count`; `marchReturned` carries `province` and `plot` (whole counts from 1, the plot the march foraged), `units` (a party, the men that went), `loot` (a `ResourceAmounts`), `occurredAt` (the return instant) and `recalled` (a required boolean, `true` when the lord recalled the march; the line is still dated by the return), with no `level`, no `unit` and no `refund`; `battleFought` carries `province` and `plot` (whole counts from 1, the camp's plot), `tier` (1, 2 or 3, the camp's tier), `won` (a required boolean), `unitsLost` (a `UnitCounts`, the lord's men lost per kind, every count possibly 0) and `campLost` (a whole count from 0, the strength the camp lost) and `occurredAt` (the arrival, the hour of the fight), with no `loot` and no `refund`; `foundingSent` and `fiefFounded` (#392) each carry `province` and `plot` (whole counts from 1, the founding's plot), `name` (a non-empty string, the new fief's name as the lord gave it) and `occurredAt` (the dispatch for `foundingSent`, the arrival for `fiefFounded`), with no unit count, no `loot` and no `refund`; `transportSent` and `transportArrived` (#417) each carry `province`, `plot` and `name` (the other fief's plot and name: the destination on `transportSent`, the origin on `transportArrived`), `cargo` (a `ResourceAmounts`, the load) and `occurredAt` (the dispatch for `transportSent`, the arrival for `transportArrived`), with no unit count, no `loot` and no `refund`. Strict objects make an extra key fail to parse instead of being stripped. `FiefChronicleSchema` is `{ events }`, at most 100.
- `ProvinceMap.ts` holds `ProvinceMapSchema`, a strict `{ kingdom, province, lastProvince, terrain, plots }`; each plot is a strict `{ plot, fief, camp, reservation }` where `fief` is `null` on a free plot or a strict `{ name, isOwn }`, `camp` is `null` or a strict `{ tier, strength }`, the tier `1`, `2` or `3` and the strength a `WholeCount` from 0, and `reservation` is `null` or a strict `{ isOwn }` on a free plot a founding march reserves (S18, #389), `isOwn` true for the viewer's own founding; no player id crosses the wire, the founder's included. `ProvinceMapRequest.ts` parses the path `{ province }` as a whole number from 1. `ApiErrorKind` includes `ProvinceNotFound`.
- `PlayerSchema` is a strict `{ id, email, emailVerified, seenHints }`, `seenHints` the `HintKind`s the player has dismissed, in `HintKindSchema` order (S22, #478). `HintKindSchema` is `peasants`, `seasons`, `queue`, `library`, `barracks`, `marches`, `fullStore`, in that order. `VerifyEmailRequest.ts` holds `VerifyEmailRequestSchema`, a strict `{ token }` with a non-empty string. `ApiErrorKind` includes `TokenInvalid` and `MailNotSent`.
- `ForgotPasswordRequest.ts` holds `ForgotPasswordRequestSchema`, a strict `{ email }`. `ResetPasswordRequest.ts` holds `ResetPasswordRequestSchema`, a strict `{ token, password }` with a non-empty token and the 8-character password rule of sign-up.
- `FiefList.ts` holds `FiefListSchema`, a strict `{ fiefs }`, each a strict `{ id, name, coordinates, freeSlots, fullStores }`, the `id` a uuid, `coordinates` a strict `{ kingdom, province, plot }`, `freeSlots` the idle slots among `build`, `study`, `recruit` and `march` in that order, and `fullStores` the `ResourceKind`s at or above the capacity at the read (S22, #465); no player id crosses the wire. Each `FiefOverview.resources.<kind>` carries `fullAt`, the ISO instant the store reaches the capacity, or `null` when it never does. `FiefOverview.id` is the fief's uuid. `FiefRequest.ts` holds `FiefRequestSchema`, the path `{ fiefId }`, a uuid; a plain `z.object`, so a mounted route's `c.req.param()` with other params still parses, and the other path schemas stay plain `z.object` for the same reason.
- `FiefContent.fiefCap` is a whole count from 1, the fiefs a lord may hold (2 in the shipped content, S18). `DispatchFoundingRequestSchema` is a strict `{ province, plot, name }`, `province` and `plot` whole counts from 1 and `name` a non-empty string (a blank name parses and the domain answers `BlankFiefName`). `FiefOverview.march` gains a third variant, `order: 'found'`, with `name` (the new fief's name), `stayHours` 0, `camp: null` and `fought: false`. `ApiErrorKind` includes `FiefCapReached` and `PlotReserved`.
- `DispatchTransportRequestSchema` (S19, #412) is a strict `{ toFiefId, units, cargo }`: `toFiefId` a uuid, `units` a `PartySchema`, `cargo` a `ResourceAmountsSchema` (whole amounts from 0; all five at 0 parses and the domain refuses it `EmptyCargo`). `MarchStateSchema` gains the `transport` variant: `toFiefId`, `cargo`, `stayHours` 0, `camp: null`, `fought: false`. `ApiErrorKindSchema` gains `EmptyCargo` and `CargoAboveCarry`.
- `FiefOverview.incomingCargo` (S19, #415) is `null` or a strict `{ fromFiefId, from, cargo, departedAt, arrivesAt }`: `fromFiefId` the origin's uuid, `from` a strict `{ name, province, plot }` of the origin as the destination stored it at dispatch (`name` non-empty, `province` and `plot` whole counts from 1), `cargo` a `ResourceAmounts`, `departedAt` (the transport's departure, since #502) and `arrivesAt` ISO instants. A fief has at most one cargo on its way.
- `FiefContent.goals` (S22, #468) is an ordered array of strict `{ building, level }`, `level` a whole count from 1; required, so content without it fails the parse. `FiefOverview.goal` is `null` when every goal is met or the fief's guidance is dismissed, otherwise a strict `{ position, count, building, level, state, missing }` discriminated on `state`: `pending` with `missing` a strict `{ resources, peasants }`, `resources` an array of strict `{ resource, amount }` (each amount above 0, the short resources only) and `peasants` a whole count; `underway` with `missing: null`. `position` and `count` are whole counts from 1.
- `FiefContent.digest` (S22, #470) is a strict `{ absenceSeconds }`, a whole count from 1; required. `Digest.ts` holds `DigestSchema`, a strict `{ acknowledgedAt, isDue, fiefs }`: `acknowledgedAt` an ISO instant, `isDue` a boolean, and `fiefs` an array of strict `{ id, name, events, stores }`, `id` a uuid, `name` non-empty, `events` an array of `FiefEventSchema`, and `stores` an array of strict `{ resource, fullSince }`, a `ResourceKind` and the ISO instant it filled. No player id crosses the wire.
