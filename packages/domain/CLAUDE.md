# packages/domain

The game rules as pure TypeScript (ADR 004). Everything else depends on this package; it depends on nothing.

## Commands

- `pnpm --filter @mygame/domain typecheck` — `tsc --noEmit` over `src` and the Vitest config.
- `pnpm --filter @mygame/domain test` — Vitest, runs `src/**/*.test.ts`.
- `pnpm gate` from the repo root before every commit.

## Entry

`src/index.ts` is the only entry. Consumers import from `@mygame/domain`, never from a path under `src`.

## Gotchas

- Zero runtime dependencies. `typescript` and `vitest` are dev dependencies taken from the workspace `catalog:`; `jq '.dependencies' package.json` prints `null`.
- No `node:` imports, no framework, no `zod`. The leak check in `.claude/rules/architecture.md` must stay empty.
- No comments of any kind, including JSDoc. The narrow exceptions are in `.claude/rules/typescript-style.md`.
- Explicit return type on every exported function, method and exported arrow.
- Nothing throws. A failure is a member of the `DomainError` union returned inside a `Result`; `rg -n "extends Error|throw new" src` stays empty, test files included. In a test, narrow a `Result` with Vitest's `assert(result.ok)` instead of throwing.
- Time comes only from the `Clock` port. `Date.now()`, `new Date()`, `setTimeout` and `setInterval` never appear in `src`, tests included; a test builds a `frozenClock` beside itself.
- `Instant` is stored as epoch milliseconds; `Duration` is whole or fractional seconds and never negative.
- A busy `BuildSlot` carries `cost`, the stocks the enqueue debited, taken from the same catalog line as the debit. Whatever needs what a running upgrade cost reads it there, never the catalog, which may have changed since the enqueue (N5).
- `Fief` carries its `buildQueue`, the entries waiting behind the slot in order, each with the building, target level, debited cost and duration fixed at the enqueue (ADR 011). `Fief.restore` reads no catalog, so it cannot check the content cap `FiefSettings.buildQueueCap`; it refuses an entry whose duration is negative (`NegativeDuration`) or not whole seconds (`FractionalDuration`).
- `Fief.enqueueUpgrade(entry, stocksAtNow, now, buildQueueCap)` is the only way an upgrade is enqueued: an idle slot with nothing waiting takes it, otherwise it joins the end of the queue, and a full queue refuses `QueueFull` (`Fief.roomForUpgrade`). Either way it debits the cost at `now`. `enqueueBuilding` checks the room first, then judges the target level, `MaxLevelReached` and the peasants on `Fief.projectedBuildingLevels` (built levels with the slot and every waiting entry applied) through `deriveProjectedFreePeasants`, the free count of `derivePeasantCounts(levels, units, recruitOrder, catalog)`, which answers the peasant trio (supplied, occupied, free) at any building levels, adds each unit count, and an open recruit order's `count`, times `FiefSettings.units[kind].peasantOccupancy` to the occupied peasants (`FiefUnitCounts`, every kind at 0 on `found`, restored through its invariant factory or refused with `InvalidUnitCount`), and is what the overview reads for both the built and the projected counts.
- `Fief.cancelUpgrade(target, stocksAtNow, now, catalog)` is the only upgrade cancel. It answers a `ChangedFief` (`{ fief, events }`): one `upgradeCancelled` for the named entry, then one per entry dropped in cascade in queue order, each refunding the cost it stored, all at the cancel instant (`revalidateBuildQueue` answers the `dropped` entries; `refundOf` sums them); the `cancelUpgrade` use case answers the same. `target` is an `UpgradeTarget` (`building`, `targetLevel`), unique across the slot and the queue, so a stale screen can never cancel a neighbour. It refuses `UpgradeNotFound` when the slot has finished by `now` (`isSlotFinishedBy`, the one predicate `resolveUpgrade` shares) or when neither the slot nor the queue holds the target. It refunds the entry's stored cost, then revalidates the remaining entries with `entryFitsProjection` from the built levels plus the slot still busy, refunding every entry dropped in cascade, and starts the first kept entry at `now` when the slot was the one cancelled.
- Known gap: if the catalog loses a level after its enqueue, `derivePeasantsForUpgrade` answers `UnknownBuildingLevel` and every read of that fief fails; the enqueue projection and the cancel cascade share it.
- `scheduleBuildQueue(slot, buildQueue)` chains the waiting entries: the first starts at the slot's `finishesAt`, each next one when the one before finishes. Behind an idle slot it schedules nothing.
- `resolveUpgrade` first restarts a build queue left behind an idle slot (`Fief.isSlotIdleWithQueue`, which only a cancel stored before #92 left behind). `Fief.resumeBuildQueue(catalog)` revalidates the entries in order against the projection with `entryFitsProjection`: the target level must be the projected level plus one, and the peasant increase must fit the projected free peasants, read through `derivePeasantCounts`. A projection with more occupied than supplied peasants does not fit, so the entry is dropped, while the enqueue refuses the same case with `NegativeFreePeasants`. Every entry that no longer fits is dropped, and its cost is refunded to the stocks at `storedAt`. The first entry that fits starts at `storedAt`, the cancel instant. It then walks one timeline: while the earliest finish among the busy build slot, the busy study slot, the open recruit order's end (`recruitOrderEndsAt`, `startedAt + count × perUnitSeconds`), an attack's battle (an `AttackMarch` neither recalled nor `fought`, at `marchInstantsOf(march).arrivesAt`), a founding's arrival (a `FoundingMarch` not recalled, at its `arrivesAt`) and an away march's return (`marchInstantsOf(march).returnsAt`) is at or before `now`, it materializes the stocks to that finish at the building and art levels in force. `Fief.completeUpgrade` applies an upgrade finish (the level, and the first waiting entry moves into the slot, started at that finish and finishing its stored duration later) and `Fief.completeStudy` applies a study finish (the art rises to `targetLevel`, the study slot idles) and `Fief.completeRecruitOrder` closes an ended order (its `count` moves into the unit counts, the recruit slot idles, so the occupied peasants do not change) and `Fief.completeMarch` closes a returned march (its `loot` is credited to the stocks materialized to `returnsAt`, even above the capacity, where they freeze; `storedAt = returnsAt`; the march slot idles; no unit count and no peasant changes, since the units away were counted all along) and `Fief.completeBattle(attack, battle, stocksAtArrival)` fights an attack at its arrival (`battleOf` over the march's `units` and the snapshot stored at dispatch: each kind falls by its `unitsLost`, a win keeps the march away with `units` the survivors per kind and `fought` true, so its `marchReturned` counts them and the units at home stay right, a loss idles the march slot and writes no `marchReturned`; `storedAt = arrivesAt`). A battle during an open recruit order first settles the order's delivered units into the stored count and shrinks the order to the rest (`count` less the delivered, `startedAt` pushed by delivered × the stored `perUnitSeconds`, `cost` scaled per unit), so the stored count never goes negative, the remaining deliveries keep their instants, and the order's closing `recruitsDelivered` counts only that rest. The dead free their peasants through the count; nothing revalidates the build queue, since the free count only rises. Each battle is recorded through `camps.record` (`CampRegistry`) after `fiefs.save` and before the chronicle, as the fief's `kingdom`, the march's `province` and `plot`, the camp's strength less `campLost` (0 on a win) and `foughtAt = arrivesAt`; a recalled attack fights nothing and records nothing. `resolveUpgrade` takes `camps`, and every call site passes it. A founding arrives (S18, #387): before the walk, `resolveUpgrade` builds the new fief with `fiefFoundedBy` (`fief/fiefFoundedBy.ts`: `Fief.found` with `ids.newId()`, the march's `name`, the origin's kingdom and `playerId`, the march's province and plot, the content's `startingStocks` and `at = arrivesAt`) and saves it through `fiefs.save`, so it accrues from the arrival; the walk then applies `Fief.completeFounding(founding, stocksAtArrival)` at `arrivesAt`: the open recruit order settles as at a battle, the settler leaves the unit counts (its 4 peasants free by derivation), the march slot idles and `storedAt = arrivesAt`. When that save answers `CoordinatesTaken` (a fief holds the plot, only a sign-up that read its plots before the dispatch), nothing is founded and `Fief.turnFoundingHome(founding)` stores `recalledAt = arrivesAt` before the walk, so the settler walks home and the resolve closes it at `returnsAt` with a `marchReturned` recalled; `Fief.restore` accepts a founding's `recalledAt` equal to its `leavesAt`, its arrival, and refuses one after it. The founding writes the new fief before the origin is saved and answers no event until #388. `resolveUpgrade` takes `ids` (`IdGenerator`), and every call site passes it; the api's dry run passes a `save` that stores nothing, so it founds nothing. On a tie the upgrade applies first, then the study, then the order, then the battle, then the founding, then the march's return, and their events follow that order, so a `marchReturned` comes last among the events of a tied instant and a `battleFought` follows every upgrade, study and order finishing at its arrival. The early return checks the six through `earliestFinishedOf`, and `isSlotFinishedBy` takes either busy slot. An order still delivering is never touched: `deliveredUnitsOf(order, at)` is `min(count, floor(elapsed / perUnitSeconds))`, derived on read and never stored, and `Fief.unitCountsAt(at)` answers the stored counts plus those delivered units. Each walk step floors the stocks, so an order closing between two finishes can cost a unit per resource, as any finish does. `materializeStocks` splits every span at each season boundary it crosses, the epoch included (`nextSeasonBoundaryAfter` in `season/`), and accrues each segment with `accrue` at `deriveResourceRates` for the segment's start: k boundaries make k + 1 segments, each floored and clamped on its own, so a read may land up to one unit per boundary below the unfloored sum. A finish and a boundary order themselves by instant, since each walk step is one more materialization. The walk stops at the first upgrade still building; with nothing left the slot is idle. It accrues to `now`, or to the fief's `storedAt` when that is later. `hasChanged` is true when the read restarted the queue, completed at least one upgrade or study, closed an order, fought a battle, founded or turned home a founding, or closed a march. `ResolvedFief` is `ChangedFief & { hasChanged }`: one event per finish the walk applied, in walk order (upgrade first on a tie), each at its `finishesAt`, never `now`; `events: []` when `hasChanged` is false, one `recruitsDelivered` per closed order, one `battleFought` per battle, one `marchReturned` per closed march, and no event for the entries `resumeBuildQueue` drops.
- `BuildingKind` has seven members; `library` levels are a `LibraryLevel` and `barracks` levels a `BarracksLevel` (cost, duration, occupancy, no effect), so every `FiefBuildingLevels` fixture carries a `library` and a `barracks` key. The built barracks level gates and divides recruiting (below).
- `Fief` carries `studySlot` (`StudySlot`, idle on `found`): idle, or busy with `art`, `targetLevel`, `startedAt`, `finishesAt` and the `cost` it debited, mirroring the busy `BuildSlot`. `Fief.restore` refuses a busy study whose target level is below 1 (`InvalidArtLevel`), that finishes before `storedAt`, that starts after it finishes, or that holds a negative cost, with the same members as the build slot (`validateTimedWork`). Every `StoredFief` fixture carries a `studySlot` key.
- `Fief` carries `recruitOrder` (`RecruitOrder`, idle on `found`): idle, or open with `unit`, `count`, the `cost` it debited (the unit's cost times `count`), `perUnitSeconds` and `startedAt`; one order at a time, no queue. `Fief.restore` refuses an open order whose `count` is not a whole count from 1 (`InvalidUnitCount`), whose `perUnitSeconds` is not (`InvalidUnitDuration`), whose cost is negative (`NegativeResourceAmount`), or that ends before `storedAt` (`SlotFinishesBeforeStored`, with the order's end as `finishesAt`). Every `StoredFief` fixture carries a `recruitOrder` key. `Fief.placeRecruitOrder({ unit, count, terms }, stocksAtNow, now, trainPercent)` is the only way an order opens: it refuses `InvalidUnitCount` (below 1 or fractional), then `BarracksNotBuilt` (built barracks at 0), then `BarracksTooLow { unit, requiredBarracksLevel, barracksLevel }` (built barracks below the unit's `terms.barracksLevel`, the shape of `LibraryLevelTooLow`), then `RecruitSlotBusy`, then `InsufficientResources`; it debits at `now` and fixes `perUnitSeconds = deriveUnitDurationSeconds(terms.durationSeconds, barracksLevel, trainPercent)`, `ceil(durationSeconds * trainPercent / (100 * (1 + barracksLevel)))` with one rounding, at the built level, never the projected one. The `Fief` reads no calendar: the use case hands it the `train` percent of `durationPercentAt` at `clock.now()`, as `startStudy` hands the study percent, and that one duration holds for every unit of the order; an order that runs into another season is never retimed, since the resolve reads the stored `perUnitSeconds`. The use case `placeRecruitOrder({ playerId, fiefId, unit, count })` then refuses `NotEnoughPeasants` when `count` times the unit's occupancy exceeds `deriveLowestFreePeasants`, the lowest free count across the built levels and every step of the slot and the queue in order, since an order sized to the built and the projected ends alone can drive the built trio negative when a busy upgrade lands before a queued farm (#339). The order occupies every unit's peasants from the instant it is placed, delivered or not; the build slot, the build queue and the study slot are untouched. Delivery and its close are in the resolve gotcha; the api stores the counts in `fief_units` and the open order in `fief_recruit_orders`.
- Known gap: neither `Fief.restore` nor `completeStudy` checks a study's `targetLevel` against the current art level or the catalog; a stored study that targets a level the catalog lacks makes every read after its finish fail with `UnknownArtLevel`, like the building gap above.
- `Fief.startStudy(line, stocksAtNow, now, studyPercent)` is the only way a study starts. It refuses `StudySlotBusy` (a study runs), then `LibraryLevelTooLow` (the built `library` level is below the line's `requiredLibraryLevel`, level 0 included), then `InsufficientResources`. It debits the line's cost at `now` and touches neither the build slot, the build queue nor the peasants. The use case `startStudy` reads `nextArtLevelOf(art, level, catalog)` first and answers `ArtMaxLevelReached` when there is no next line. `deriveStudyDurationSeconds(durationSeconds, libraryLevel, studyPercent)` is `ceil(durationSeconds * studyPercent / (100 * (1 + libraryLevel)))` at the built library level, never the projected one: one division and one `ceil`, never a rounding per factor (1000 s at library 2 in a 75 % winter is 250, where two roundings give 251). The use case hands `Fief.startStudy` the study percent of `durationPercentAt(now, settings)`, so the `Fief` reads no calendar; the overview reads the same function at `storedAt`. `deriveBuildDurationSeconds(durationSeconds, buildPercent)` is `ceil(durationSeconds * buildPercent / 100)`; `enqueueBuilding` stores it on the entry with the build percent at `now`, whether the entry takes the slot or waits, and nothing retimes a stored entry or a running study when the season turns (ADR 011). A positive duration never scales below 1 second; a duration of 0 stays 0. `artLevelInForce(art, level, catalog)` answers the line in force (`undefined` at level 0, `UnknownArtLevel` for a missing or mismatched line), read by `deriveResourceRates` and the overview. `artResourceOf(art, catalog)` answers the resource the art's level-1 line raises (`UnknownArtLevel` with `level: 1` when that line is missing or mismatched), read by the overview; both share the guard in `artLineOf`.
- `Fief.cancelStudy(target, stocksAtNow, now)` is the only study cancel. It and the `cancelStudy` use case answer a `ChangedFief` with one `studyCancelled` refunding the study slot's stored cost at the cancel instant. `target` is a `StudyTarget` (`art`, `targetLevel`). It refuses `StudyNotFound` when the study slot is idle, holds another art or level, or has finished by `now` (`isSlotFinishedBy`). It credits the slot's stored `cost` to `stocksAtNow`, even above the capacity, stamps `storedAt = now` and idles the study slot; no cascade, and the build slot, the build queue and the art levels are untouched. The use case `cancelStudy({ playerId, fiefId, art, targetLevel })` materializes the stocks at `now` first.
- `Fief.cancelRecruitOrder(target, stocksAtNow, now)` is the only recruit cancel. `target` is a `RecruitOrderTarget` (`unit`, `startedAt`). It refuses `RecruitOrderNotFound { unit, startedAt }` when the recruit slot is idle, holds another unit or another `startedAt`, or the order has ended by `now` (`recruitOrderEndsAt` at or before `now`, left for the resolve to close). Otherwise it adds `deliveredUnitsOf(order, now)` to the unit's stored count, so a delivery at exactly `now` is kept, credits `cost × (count − delivered) / count` per resource to `stocksAtNow`, even above the capacity and with no rounding (the stored cost is `count` times the unit cost, since seasons scale only durations), stamps `storedAt = now` and idles the recruit slot. The undelivered units' peasants are freed by derivation, nothing stored; no cascade, and the build slot, the build queue, the study slot and the levels are untouched. It and the `cancelRecruitOrder({ playerId, fiefId, unit, startedAt })` use case answer a `ChangedFief` with one `recruitsCancelled` at the cancel instant, with the units delivered, `cancelled = count − delivered` and the refund it credited; the use case materializes the stocks at `now` first and records like `cancelStudy`.
- Known gap: `cancelUpgrade`, `enqueueBuilding`, `startStudy`, `cancelStudy`, `placeRecruitOrder` and `cancelRecruitOrder` open the same way (load the fief, `FiefNotFound`, `materializeStocks` at `now`, mutate, save), six copies awaiting one shared opening.
- `FiefEvent` (`fief/FiefEvent.ts`) is a union on `kind`: `upgradeFinished` (`building`, `level`, `occurredAt`), `artLearned` (`art`, `level`, `occurredAt`), `upgradeCancelled` and `studyCancelled` (the same plus `refund`, a `Stocks`), and `recruitsDelivered` (`unit`, `count`, `occurredAt`), which the resolve answers once per order it closes, with the order's `count`, stamped with its last delivery (`recruitOrderEndsAt`), never per unit delivered, and `recruitsCancelled` (`unit`, `delivered`, `cancelled`, `occurredAt`, `refund`), which the recruit cancel answers once, at the cancel instant, and `marchReturned` (`province`, `plot`, `units`, a count per kind, `loot`, `recalled`, `occurredAt`), which the resolve answers once per march it closes, stamped with `returnsAt`, never while the march is away, with the stored `loot` and `recalled` true exactly when the closed march holds a `recalledAt`, and `battleFought` (`province`, `plot`, `tier`, `won`, `unitsLost`, a count per kind, `campLost`, `occurredAt`), which the resolve answers once per battle it fights, stamped with the attack's `arrivesAt`, with the camp's `tier` read at dispatch and the `Battle`'s outcome; a won attack answers its `marchReturned` later, at the return, with the survivors and the loot, a lost one answers none, and a recalled attack answers no battle. `foundingSent` (`province`, `plot`, `name`, `occurredAt`) is the first dispatch event: `dispatchFounding` records it on the origin at the dispatch instant, after `fiefs.save`, with the name trimmed. `fiefFounded` (the same fields) is answered by the resolve once per founding it applies, stamped with the founding's `arrivesAt`, among the origin's events in walk order; the resolve also records the same event on the new fief's id, its first event, after the origin's `record`. A founding that turns home at a held plot answers no `fiefFounded`, only the `marchReturned` of its return. `name` is the new fief's name. `level` is the level reached or the target level cancelled. A refused call answers an error and no event. `ChronicleWriter` (`ports/ChronicleWriter.ts`) receives them: `resolveUpgrade`, `cancelUpgrade`, `cancelStudy`, `cancelRecruitOrder` and `dispatchFounding` take `chronicle` and call `record(fief.id, events)` right after `fiefs.save` succeeds, never when they save nothing. The domain never reads the chronicle. Tests pass `inMemoryChronicle()` (`testing/inMemoryChronicle.ts`, `recordedEventsOf(fiefId)`) or `refusingChronicle()`.
- `BuildingCatalog.artLevelOf(art, level)` answers an `ArtLevel` (`art`, `level`, `cost`, `durationSeconds`, `requiredLibraryLevel`, `resource`, `ratePercent`) or `undefined`. Every `BuildingCatalog` fake in the domain and api tests must implement it; `() => undefined` suffices where no art is studied.
- `Fief` carries `artLevels` (`FiefArtLevels`, with every `ArtKind` at 0 on `found`). `Fief.restore` refuses an art level that is negative or not whole with `InvalidArtLevel`. `artKinds` derives from a `Record<ArtKind, true>`, so a new art does not compile until it is listed.
- `deriveResourceRates(buildingLevels, artLevels, terrain, catalog, at)` sums base, producers and terrain. Then it collects the whole percents that scale each resource: `100 + ratePercent` of each art above level 0 on its line's `resource`, then, last, the percent of the season in force at `at` for every resource, so the season scales the terrain bonus and the arts too; before the epoch there is no season percent. Each rate is scaled once, as `rate * (product of its percents) / 100 ** count`: never `rate * (1 + ratePercent / 100)`, which falls short in floating point (200/h at 15 % would give 229.99…), and never one division per percent, which drifts too (31/h with a 5 % art in a 125 % season would give 40.68749…, not 40.6875). A missing or mismatched art line answers `UnknownArtLevel`. Level 0 reads no line. `materializeStocks` derives the rates anew at the start of each season segment (see the resolve gotcha).
- `FiefSettings.seasons` is a `SeasonCalendar` (`season/SeasonCalendar.ts`): `epoch` (an `Instant`), `daysPerSeason`, `multiplierPercent`, a whole percent per `SeasonKind` and resource, and `durationPercent`, a `DurationPercent` (`{ build, study, train, road }`, whole percents) per `SeasonKind`. `durationPercentAt(instant, settings)` (`season/durationPercentAt.ts`) answers the `DurationPercent` in force, `{ build: 100, study: 100, train: 100, road: 100 }` before the epoch; rates never read it and durations never read `multiplierPercent`. `seasonAt(instant, settings)` (`season/seasonAt.ts`) answers the `Season` in force (`kind`, `year`, `endsAt`) or `undefined` before the epoch: spring of year 1 starts at the epoch, the seasons run spring, summer, autumn, winter, and a season runs from its start, included, to its `endsAt`, excluded. Every `FiefSettings` fixture carries `seasons`; `testing/neutralSeasons.ts` is a calendar with every cell at 100, for tests that are not about seasons.
- `FiefSettings.forage` is `ForageTerms` (`secondsPerProvince`, `secondsPerPlot`, `maxStayHours`, `yieldPerHour` per `Terrain` and resource); the carry is the unit's, `UnitTerms.carry`. `marchOneWaySeconds(from, to, units, terms, seasonRoadPercent)` and `forageLootOf(terrain, units, stayHours, terms, lootPercent)` (`MarchTerms`, the settings' `forage` and `units`; `units` a `UnitCountsByKind`, the count sent per kind; `lootPercent` a `LootPercent`, a whole percent per `ResourceKind`) in `march/` read no clock and no season: the caller hands them the percents. The road is `ceil(base × roadPercentOf(units, terms.units) × seasonRoadPercent / 10000)`, one rounding over the product (61 s of riders in a 75 % autumn is 23 s, where two roundings give 24), `base` the provinces and plots crossed and `roadPercentOf` the highest `roadPercent` among the kinds with a count from 1, so the slowest kind sent times the march. The loot is `floor(heads × rate × ms × percent / (3_600_000 × 100))` per yielded resource, one `floor` over the product, `heads` the total over the kinds and `percent` the resource's `lootPercent`, each capped at `floor(carryOf(units, terms.units) / yielded)`, `carryOf` the summed `nₖ × carryₖ`, `yielded` the resources the terrain yields above 0, and never forages gold whatever the terms say. Every `FiefSettings` fixture carries `forage`; `testing/plainForage.ts` holds the shipped terms.
- `Fief` carries `march` (`March` in `march/March.ts`, idle on `found`): idle, or away with `province`, `plot`, `units` (the party, a `UnitCountsByKind`), `stayHours`, `departedAt`, `oneWaySeconds`, the `loot` and the `lootPercent` fixed at dispatch; one march at a time. `lootPercent` is a `LootPercent`, a whole percent per `ResourceKind`, gold included, the shape of `multiplierPercent`. The season is fixed at dispatch and never retimed (ADR 017): `Fief.dispatchMarch` and `Fief.dispatchAttack` take a `MarchSeason` (`roadPercent`, `lootPercent`) and read no calendar; the use cases hand them `marchSeasonAt(now, settings)` (`season/marchSeasonAt.ts`), the `road` of `durationPercentAt` and `multiplierPercentAt` (`season/multiplierPercentAt.ts`, the season's `multiplierPercent`, 100 for each resource before the epoch) at the one `clock.now()` they read. Both store the season's percents as `lootPercent`; an attack's road is shortened too, while its battle and `attackLootOf` read no season. `Fief.restore` refuses a `lootPercent` below 1 or fractional with `InvalidLootPercent { resource, percent }`, naming the first resource in `resourceKinds` order (`resources/resourceKinds.ts`). A party is valid when every kind's count is a whole count from 0 and at least one is from 1; `refuseInvalidParty(units)` (`march/refuseInvalidParty.ts`) refuses `InvalidUnitCount` naming the first kind, in `unitKinds` order, that is negative or fractional, or the first kind with count 0 when every count is 0. `Fief.restore` refuses an away march whose party is not valid (`InvalidUnitCount`), whose `stayHours` is not (`StayOutOfRange`), whose `oneWaySeconds` is negative or fractional (`NegativeDuration`, `FractionalDuration`) or whose loot is negative (`NegativeResourceAmount`); it does not check the units away against the counts. An away march that returns before `storedAt` is refused too (`SlotFinishesBeforeStored`, with `returnsAt` as `finishesAt`). Every `StoredFief` fixture carries a `march` key. `marchInstantsOf(march)` answers `arrivesAt = departedAt + oneWaySeconds`, `leavesAt = arrivesAt + stayHours × 3600` and `returnsAt = leavesAt + oneWaySeconds`; `marchPhaseAt(march, at)` answers `outbound`, `foraging` or `returning`, an instant equal to a boundary reading the later phase. Both derive on read and nothing is stored per phase; the resolve closes the march at `returnsAt`. `Fief.unitsAtHomeAt(at)` is `unitCountsAt(at)` less each kind of an away march's `units`; the units away keep their peasants (ADR 019). `MarchOrder` and `AttackOrder` carry `units`. `Fief.roomForMarch(order, maxStayHours)` refuses `InvalidUnitCount` (`refuseInvalidParty`), then `StayOutOfRange` (outside 1..`maxStayHours`), then `MarchSlotBusy`; `Fief.dispatchMarch(order, now, terms, season)` (`MarchTerms`) checks the room again, refuses `NotEnoughUnitsAtHome { unit, count, atHome }` for the first kind, in `unitKinds` order, sent above its units at home, and stores the march with `departedAt = now`, `marchOneWaySeconds` from the fief's coordinates over the party and `forageLootOf` over the party on `terrainOf(province)`. The use case `dispatchMarch({ playerId, fiefId, province, plot, units, stayHours })` takes `fiefs`, `map`, `catalog` and `clock` and refuses in order: the room, `MarchTargetOutOfBounds` (a province outside 1..`lastOccupiedProvince + 1` or a plot outside 1..`plotsPerProvince`), `MarchToOwnPlot` (`holdersIn` lists the plot under the sending player, so any fief of the lord, not only the sending one; a transport names its destination by id and never passes this check, #408 Decision 1), `PlotHeld` (`holdersIn` lists it under another lord), `PlotReserved { province, plot }` (`reservationsIn` lists it, whoever founds there), all four in `march/refuseUnreachableTarget.ts`, the one function a forage, an attack and a founding share, then `PlotHasCamp { province, plot }` (`campOf` answers a camp), then `NotEnoughUnitsAtHome`. No debit, no stamp of `storedAt`: the units away stay counted and keep their peasants, and the build slot, the build queue, the study slot and the recruit slot are untouched. The api stores an away march in `fief_marches`, one row per fief, and no row is an idle march slot.
- `AwayMarch` is a union on `order`: a `ForageMarch` (`order: 'forage'`) or an `AttackMarch` (`order: 'attack'`), which adds `camp` (`AttackedCamp`, `{ tier, strength }`, the snapshot read at dispatch) and `fought` (false at dispatch). An attack holds `stayHours` 0, so `marchInstantsOf` answers `leavesAt = arrivesAt` and `marchPhaseAt` reads it `returning` from its arrival with no code of its own. `Fief.restore` refuses a forage stay below 1 and an attack stay other than 0 (`StayOutOfRange`), and an attack camp whose tier is outside 1..3 or whose strength is negative or fractional (`InvalidCamp { tier, strength }`); a forage march cannot hold a camp, the type forbids it. `Fief.roomForAttack({ province, plot, units })` (`AttackOrder`; `MarchOrder` stays the forage command) refuses `InvalidUnitCount` (`refuseInvalidParty`), then `MarchSlotBusy`; `Fief.dispatchAttack(order, camp, now, terms, season)` (`AttackTerms`, the settings' `forage`, `camps` and `units`) checks the room again, refuses `NotEnoughUnitsAtHome`, and stores the attack with `departedAt = now`, the road of `marchOneWaySeconds` over the party, the camp and the loot `attackLootOf` gives the survivors `battleOf` predicts, 0 for a predicted loss; the whole outcome is fixed at dispatch. The use case `dispatchAttack({ playerId, fiefId, province, plot, units })` takes `fiefs`, `map`, `camps` (a `CampRegistry`), `catalog` and `clock` and refuses, writing nothing, in order: the room (so `InvalidUnitCount` comes before any battle is computed), `MarchTargetOutOfBounds`, `MarchToOwnPlot`, `PlotHeld`, `PlotReserved`, `PlotHasNoCamp { province, plot }` (`campOf` answers nothing), then `NotEnoughUnitsAtHome`; the camp's strength is `campStrengthAt` at `now` over `camps.lastBattleOf(address)`. A camp at strength 0 can be attacked: a win with nothing lost and nothing brought. No debit, no unit or peasant change, no stamp of `storedAt`. The api passes `camps` from the transaction's stores (#302), since the resolve before it may have just recorded a battle on that camp. `Fief.recallMarch` recalls an attack only outbound, with its loot at 0; at or after its arrival it answers `MarchAlreadyReturning`. The resolve fights the battle at the arrival (the resolve gotcha); the api stores the attack and its camp battles (#298).
- A `FoundingMarch` (`order: 'found'`, S18, #384) is the third `AwayMarch`: `name`, the new fief's name trimmed by `FiefName`, `units` exactly `foundingParty` (`march/foundingParty.ts`, 1 settler and nothing else), `stayHours` 0, `loot` 0 and the season's `lootPercent`. `Fief.restore` refuses a stored founding whose stay is not 0 (`StayOutOfRange`) or whose party is not one settler alone (`InvalidUnitCount` naming the first kind, in `unitKinds` order, that differs from `foundingParty`). `Fief.roomForFounding()` refuses `MarchSlotBusy`; `Fief.dispatchFounding({ province, plot, name }, now, terms, season)` checks the room again, refuses `NotEnoughUnitsAtHome { unit: 'settler', count: 1, atHome }` and stores the march with `departedAt = now` and the road of `marchOneWaySeconds` over the settler at the season's `roadPercent`. The use case `dispatchFounding({ playerId, fiefId, province, plot, name })` takes `fiefs`, `map`, `catalog` and `clock` and refuses, writing nothing, in order: `FiefNotFound`, `BlankFiefName`, `MarchSlotBusy`, `FiefCapReached { cap }` (`fiefsOf(playerId)` plus `foundingsOnTheRoadOf(playerId)` at or above `FiefSettings.fiefCap`), `MarchTargetOutOfBounds`, `MarchToOwnPlot`, `PlotHeld`, `PlotReserved`, `PlotHasCamp`, then `NotEnoughUnitsAtHome`. A founding is recalled as any march: outbound only, with no loot, and `MarchAlreadyReturning` from its arrival, since its stay is 0. An unrecalled founding founds the fief at its arrival (the resolve gotcha). A founding neither recalled nor resolved reserves its plot from dispatch (#378 Decision 9, #386): `refuseUnreachableTarget` refuses a forage, an attack or another founding to it with `PlotReserved`, and `occupiedPlots()` counts it, so sign-up never places a lord there; a recall frees it. The api's `save` still answers `PlotReserved` from its unique index when two foundings race past the read; the domain's in-memory repository does not check it, though it refuses a second fief on a held plot with `CoordinatesTaken`, as the adapters do. A sign-up that reads its plots just before a founding is dispatched to the lowest free plot can still take it; the founding then turns home at its arrival. Known gap: a reservation outlives its arrival until a read of the founder's fiefs applies it (ADR 005).
- An `AwayMarch` may carry `recalledAt`. For a recalled march `marchInstantsOf` answers `arrivesAt = min(departedAt + oneWaySeconds, recalledAt)`, `leavesAt = recalledAt` and `returnsAt = recalledAt + (arrivesAt − departedAt)`, so `marchPhaseAt` reads it `returning` from `recalledAt` and the resolve closes it at that `returnsAt` with no change of its own. `Fief.recallMarch({ departedAt }, now, terms)` (`MarchTerms`) refuses `MarchNotFound { departedAt }` when the march slot is idle or holds another `departedAt`, then `MarchAlreadyReturning` when `marchPhaseAt(march, now)` is `returning`, a recalled march included; otherwise it stores `recalledAt = now` and overwrites `loot` with `forageLootOfMilliseconds(terrain, units, foragedMilliseconds, terms, march.lootPercent)`, `foragedMilliseconds = max(0, now − arrivesAt)` in whole milliseconds: all 0 when outbound, per yielded resource `floor(heads × ratePerHour × foragedMilliseconds × percent / (3_600_000 × 100))` capped at the even share of the summed carry when foraging, never gold, `percent` the march's stored `lootPercent`, so a recall scales by the percents of its dispatch, never of the recall. `forageLootOf` is the same function over `stayHours × 3_600_000`. Stocks, `storedAt`, the unit counts and the other slots are untouched. `Fief.restore` refuses a `recalledAt` before `departedAt` (`SlotStartsAfterFinish`, `departedAt` to `recalledAt`) or at or after the unrecalled `leavesAt` (`SlotStartsAfterFinish`, `recalledAt` to `leavesAt`), except that a founding may hold `recalledAt` at its `leavesAt`, the arrival it turned home at. The use case `recallMarch({ playerId, fiefId, departedAt })` takes `fiefs`, `catalog` and `clock`, no `chronicle` and no materialization, since the recall writes no event and moves no stock; it saves nothing on a refusal. The api answers both members `internalFailure` until the recall route exists; `DrizzleFiefRepository` stores `recalledAt` in `fief_marches.recalled_at`.
- `UnitKind` is `infantry`, `cavalry` (S16) and `settler` (S18, #383). `byUnitKind(entryOf)` (`fief/byUnitKind.ts`, exported) is the one literal over the kinds: `unitKinds` and `FiefUnitCounts.none` derive from it, and the api builds every per-kind record with it, so a new kind is one entry there plus each `Record<UnitKind, …>` the compiler names. Every `units` fixture carries every kind. Riders and settlers are recruited and counted like infantry; a settler needs barracks level 5 (`BarracksTooLow { unit: 'settler', requiredBarracksLevel: 5, barracksLevel }` below it) and occupies 4 peasants. `Fief.roomForMarch` and `Fief.roomForAttack` refuse a party holding a settler with `UnitUnfitForOrder { unit: 'settler', order }` (`march/refuseUnfitUnits.ts`) right after `refuseInvalidParty` and before every other check, so `battleOf`, `carryOf` and the forage loot never see a settler; `battleOf` skips a kind the party holds none of, so a strength of 0 never divides. The road, the forage, the battle, the attack loot, the march and its events take a count per kind (#349); the api stores and answers the infantry alone until #351 and #352.
- Imports are extensionless (`'./Duration'`); the base tsconfig resolves as `Bundler` without `allowImportingTsExtensions`.
- `FiefSettings.camps` is `CampTerms` (`campFraction`, `lootPerStrength`, `tiers` a record over `CampTier` 1, 2 and 3 of `CampTierTerms` `{ maxStrength, regrowHours }`), and `UnitTerms` carries `strength`, `carry`, `roadPercent` and `barracksLevel` besides its recruit terms, `strength` and `carry` whole counts from 0 and the other two from 1 (infantry 1, 48, 100 and 1, cavalry 2, 120, 50 and 3, settler 0, 0, 100 and 5 in `testing/plainUnits.ts`); `Fief.placeRecruitOrder` reads `barracksLevel`, the road reads `roadPercent`, the forage and the attack loot `carry`, the battle `strength`. Every `FiefSettings` fixture carries `camps` (`testing/plainCamps.ts`, the shipped terms). `camp/` reads no clock, no season and no table: `campOf(address, camps)` answers `{ tier }` or `undefined` from a 32-bit integer hash of `kingdom`, `province` and `plot` (`Math.imul` mixing, no `Math.random`), a camp when the hash's top 24 bits, as a fraction of 2^24, are below `campFraction`, the tier from its low 8 bits modulo 3. `campStrengthAt(tierTerms, lastBattle, at)` answers `maxStrength` when `lastBattle` (the `strength` and `foughtAt` of a `CampBattle`) is `undefined`, else `min(maxStrength, strength + floor(maxStrength × elapsed ms / (regrowHours × 3_600_000)))`, a rate, so a camp left at 1 is full sooner than one at 0; an `at` before `foughtAt` counts no elapsed time, so it never reads below the stored strength. `battleOf(units, campStrength, terms.units)` answers a `Battle` (`won`, `unitsLost` and `survivors` per kind, `campLost`): `S = Σ nₖ × strengthₖ`, won when `S > C`, a tie to the camp. A win loses `L = ceil(C² / S)` strength points, taken in `unitKinds` order, infantry first, each kind losing `min(nₖ, ceil(rest / strengthₖ))`; when that leaves no unit, the last kind the losses reach loses one fewer, so 1 infantry and 1 rider against 2 keep the rider. A loss loses every unit and the camp `min(C − 1, ceil(S² / C))`. Everything is computed from integers, never the float ratio, which answers 5 instead of 4 at 25 against 10; with infantry alone at strength 1 every result equals the infantry-only rule it replaced. `attackLootOf(terrain, campStrength, survivors, terms)` (`AttackTerms`, `survivors` per kind) answers, for each resource the terrain forages and for gold, `floor(min(lootPerStrength × C, carryOf(survivors, units)) / (yielded + 1))`, 0 for the others. `dispatchAttack` calls them; the index exports the types and `campOf`.
- `CampRegistry` (`ports/CampRegistry.ts`) is the camps' port: `lastBattleOf(address)`, `lastBattlesIn(kingdom, province)` (the latest battle of each fought plot) and `record(battle)`. A `CampBattle` is `{ kingdom, province, plot, strength, foughtAt }`, one per battle; a plot's state is its latest `foughtAt`, a tie read by the latest record; `lastBattlesIn` answers the kingdom's province only, ordered by plot. Tests pass `inMemoryCampRegistry(battles)` (`testing/inMemoryCampRegistry.ts`), which follows the same rules as the api's `campRegistryContract`.
- `FiefRepository` (`ports/FiefRepository.ts`) keys a fief by its id: `fiefOf(fiefId)` (the api locks that row inside a transaction), `fiefsOf(playerId)` (the ids of every fief the player holds, ordered by province then plot), `holdsFief(playerId)` (sign-up only, `foundFief`), `foundingsOnTheRoadOf(playerId)` (the count of the player's founding marches neither recalled nor resolved, read by `dispatchFounding`), `occupiedPlots()` (every held plot plus the target of every founding neither recalled nor resolved, in the sending fief's kingdom, read by `foundFief` through `lowestFreeCoordinates`) and `save`. Every use case command that names a fief carries `fiefId` beside `playerId` (`FiefOfPlayer`, `fief/FiefOfPlayer.ts`), `resolveUpgrade` included, and opens with `ownFiefOf` (`fief/ownFiefOf.ts`): an unknown fief or one another player holds answers `FiefNotFound { fiefId }` before any other refusal, writing nothing. `PlayerId` and `FiefId` are both `string`, so the compiler never catches one passed for the other; `inMemoryFiefRepository` keys by `fief.id` and `storedFiefOf(fiefId)` reads by it.
- `KingdomMapReader` (`ports/KingdomMapReader.ts`) is the map's read port: `addressOf(fiefId)` (a `HeldAddress`, the fief's `address` and its holder's `playerId`, or `undefined` for an unknown id), `lastOccupiedProvince(kingdom)` (0 when no fief), `holdersIn(kingdom, province)` answering `PlotHolder`s (`plot`, `name`, `playerId`), `reservationsIn(kingdom, province)` answering a `PlotReservation` (`plot`, `playerId` of the founder) per founding march neither recalled nor resolved whose target lies there, the kingdom being the sending fief's, ordered by plot. Its methods answer plain values, not a `Result`, like `FiefRepository.occupiedPlots`. Tests pass `inMemoryKingdomMap(heldPlots, reservedPlots)` (`testing/inMemoryKingdomMap.ts`, `reservedPlots` a list of `ReservedPlot`, `{ playerId, address }`, empty when left out). Every `KingdomMapReader` fake gains each new method.
- `readProvinceMap({ playerId, fiefId, province? }, { map, catalog, camps, clock })` answers a `ProvinceMap` (`kingdom/ProvinceMap.ts`). It opens on the province of the fief it is given when no `province` is named, and refuses `FiefNotFound { fiefId }` when `addressOf` answers nothing or another player holds the fief. `camps` is only `Pick<CampRegistry, 'lastBattlesIn'>`, so a caller may hand it a registry that cannot record. Each plot answers `camp`: `{ tier, strength }` when no fief holds the plot and `campOf` places one, the strength `campStrengthAt` over the plot's entry in `lastBattlesIn(kingdom, province)` at `clock.now()`; a held plot, the own plot included, a reserved plot and a plot `campOf` passes over answer `undefined`. Each plot answers `reservation`: `{ isOwn }` when no fief holds the plot and `reservationsIn(kingdom, province)` lists it, `isOwn` true when the viewer is the founder; `undefined` otherwise, a held plot included (#389). Every fief of the viewer is marked `isOwn`. It resolves nothing and reads no stock. `lastProvince` is `lastOccupiedProvince + 1`, so the province past the last held one opens empty; a province below 1, not whole or above it refuses `ProvinceNotFound { province, lastProvince }`. Plots run 1..`plotsPerProvince`; a holder on a higher plot is not listed.
- `terrainOf(province)` takes the province number; the terrain belongs to the province, not the plot.
- A `TransportMarch` (`order: 'transport'`, S19, #412) is the fourth `AwayMarch`: `toFiefId`, the lord's other fief, and `cargo`, the `Stocks` it carries, with `province` and `plot` the destination's, `stayHours` 0, `loot` 0 and the season's `lootPercent`. `Fief.restore` refuses a stored transport holding a settler (`UnitUnfitForOrder { unit: 'settler', order: 'transport' }`), a stay (`StayOutOfRange`), a negative cargo amount (`NegativeResourceAmount`) or an empty cargo (`EmptyCargo`). `Fief` carries `incomingCargo` (`fief/IncomingCargo.ts`: `fromFiefId`, the origin's `name`, `province` and `plot`, `cargo`, `arrivesAt`), `undefined` on `found`; `StoredFief.incomingCargo` is optional, so no fixture needs it, and `Fief.restore` refuses a negative amount or an `arrivesAt` before `storedAt` (`SlotFinishesBeforeStored`). `Fief.changed` clears it only when the change names the key (`'incomingCargo' in change`).
- `dispatchTransport({ playerId, fiefId, toFiefId, units, cargo })` takes `fiefs` (`fiefOf` and `save`), `catalog` and `clock`, no map, and refuses, writing nothing, in order: `FiefNotFound`, `InvalidUnitCount`, `UnitUnfitForOrder`, `NegativeResourceAmount`, `EmptyCargo`, `MarchSlotBusy` (the four in `Fief.roomForTransport`), `MarchToOwnPlot` (`toFiefId` is the sending fief), `FiefNotFound { fiefId: toFiefId }` (unknown or another lord's, through `ownFiefOf`), then in `Fief.dispatchTransport` `NotEnoughUnitsAtHome`, `CargoAboveCarry { cargo, carry }` (the plain sum of the five amounts against `carryOf`; equal to the carry is sent) and `InsufficientResources { missing }` against the stocks materialized at `now`. It is the first order that debits: the origin's stocks lose the cargo and `storedAt = now`; the destination gains `incomingCargo` with `arrivesAt` the march's `marchInstantsOf(...).arrivesAt`; it saves the origin, then the destination, and answers the origin. `Fief.dispatchTransport` answers a `TransportDispatch` (`{ origin, destination }`).
- The resolve credits an `incomingCargo` whose `arrivesAt` is at or before `now` (`FinishedWork` kind `cargo`, `Fief.completeCargo`): the cargo is added to the stocks materialized at `arrivesAt`, even above the capacity, where they freeze, `storedAt = arrivesAt`, and `incomingCargo` clears. It is last in `earliestFinishedOf`, so on a tie it applies after every other finish of that instant. It answers no event until #414. The transport's party returns at `returnsAt` through `completeMarch` with its `loot` 0. Known gaps until #413: a recall takes the generic path (the party turns home empty, the destination still credits the cargo), and a second transport stored on a destination whose first cargo has not arrived replaces it; only a recalled transport sent again within its first road can reach that.
