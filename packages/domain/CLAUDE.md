# packages/domain

The game rules as pure TypeScript (ADR 004). Everything else depends on this package; it depends on nothing.

## Commands

- `pnpm --filter @mygame/domain typecheck` — `tsc --noEmit` over `src` and the Vitest config.
- `pnpm --filter @mygame/domain test` — Vitest, runs `src/**/*.test.ts`.
- `pnpm gate` from the repo root before every commit.

## Entry

`src/index.ts` is the single entry (`exports["."]`); the town's names sit beside the others, its rules under `src/town/`. Consumers import from `@mygame/domain`, never from a path under `src`.

## Gotchas

- Zero runtime dependencies. `typescript` and `vitest` are dev dependencies taken from the workspace `catalog:`; `jq '.dependencies' package.json` prints `null`.
- No `node:` imports, no framework, no `zod`. The leak check in `.claude/rules/architecture.md` must stay empty.
- No comments of any kind, including JSDoc. The narrow exceptions are in `.claude/rules/typescript-style.md`.
- Explicit return type on every exported function, method and exported arrow.
- Nothing throws. A failure is a member of the `DomainError` union returned inside a `Result`; `rg -n "extends Error|throw new" src` stays empty, test files included. In a test, narrow a `Result` with Vitest's `assert(result.ok)` instead of throwing.
- Time comes only from the `Clock` port. `Date.now()`, `new Date()`, `setTimeout` and `setInterval` never appear in `src`, tests included; a test builds a `frozenClock` beside itself.
- `Instant` is stored as epoch milliseconds; `Duration` is whole or fractional seconds and never negative.
- Imports are extensionless (`'./Duration'`); the base tsconfig resolves as `Bundler` without `allowImportingTsExtensions`.
- `memory/` is the FSRS-6 scheduler of ADR 036, a port of py-fsrs 6.3.2 at commit `9446cb06` with no fuzz: no port, no use case, no content; `learning/` and the learning use cases put it in the game (below). `MemoryCard.entered(enteredAt)` enters a card in `learning` at step 0 with `stability`, `difficulty`, `lastReviewAt` and `lastRating` null; `MemoryCard.restore` refuses a step that is not a whole count from 0, a stability not above 0 or a difficulty outside 1 to 10 (`InvalidMemoryCard`). `reviewMemoryCard(card, rating, at, terms)` answers the next card and refuses `ReviewBeforeLastReview`; it never checks the due instant, so a use case refuses a card not yet due. `retrievabilityOf(card, at, terms)` and `dueInstantOf(card, terms)` derive on read, nothing stored; `dueInstantOf` refuses `UnknownMemoryStep` for a learning or relearning step the terms do not hold. Whole days are floored (`wholeDaysBetween`), a review interval is rounded half to even as Python's `round` (2.5 days is 2, 3.5 is 4), and every `e ** x` is `Math.E ** x`, never `Math.exp`, as py-fsrs writes `math.e ** x`. `retainedAt` is the review instant of a Good from `review` or `relearning` (a recall) at least 7 whole days after the review before it, null after any other review. `defaultMemoryTerms` holds py-fsrs's defaults.
- `learning/` holds the lesson rules of ADR 036 (#711). A `Lesson` (`id`, `durationSeconds`, `requiredSchoolLevel`, `words`, `skill`, a `LessonSkill` `{ resource, ratePercent }` on the town's `ResourceKind`) holds `LessonWord`s (`id`, `acceptedForms`, a non-empty tuple, `gloss`, and `frames`, a tuple of the anchor `WordFrame` `{ frame, gappedFrame }`, the gapped one with each word of the lemma blanked, then the `LaterFrame`s, each with the `fromLessonId` it is written for, in the lexicon's order, ADR 044); `LessonCatalog` (`ports/LessonCatalog.ts`) answers `lessonOf(lessonId)`, `lessonOfWord(wordId)` and `isHeadword(word)` (the NGSL 1.2 list). Cards are the player's, never the town's (rule 1): `CardRepository` (`cardsOf(playerId)`, `save(playerId, held)`, a `HeldCard` `{ wordId, card }`), `PendingReviewRepository` (`pendingReviewOf`, `pendingReviewsOf(playerId)`, every pending review of the player by word id, `save`, `saveAll(playerId, pending)`, several in one write, `delete`, keyed by player and word, a `PendingReview` `{ hintShown, nearMissAnswer, shownFrame }`, `shownFrame` the text of the frame the review first showed, null until a read shows one; a save never replaces a stored `shownFrame`, only the hint and the near-miss, and `inMemoryPendingReviews` keeps it the same way) and `ReviewLog` (`append(entry)`, append-only, never read by the domain). Tests pass `inMemoryCards`, `inMemoryPendingReviews`, `inMemoryReviewLog`, `inMemoryLessons` and `testing/lessonOne.ts`.
- `dueCardsAt(cards, now, terms)` answers the cards whose `dueInstantOf` is at or before `now`, in due order, a tie by word id. `reviewFormatOf(card)` is `recognition` in `learning`, `recall` otherwise (rule 2). `recognitionChoicesOf(wordId, lesson, card, held)` answers the word and at most 4 others of its lesson the player holds a card for, so a held-out gloss is never offered, the 4 with the lowest hash, then all ordered by hash, a tie by word id; the hash mixes each word id with the card's key instant (`lastReviewAt`, else `enteredAt`) through `mixed32` (`hash/mixed32.ts`), so the order holds across reads and moves after each review (rule 3). Each `RecognitionChoice` is `{ choiceId, wordId }`: the choice id is the 8 hex digits of the word's hash, re-mixed through `mixed32` until no earlier choice of the card holds it, so it is unique within the card, never a word id, and changes with the key instant; the api sends only the choice id and the gloss. The ids are opaque and deterministic, not secret: they guard against a client bug leaking the answer, not against a modified client (ADR 036 rule 3). The stage and the frames (#1006, ADR 044): `lemmaWordsOf(lemma)` is the lemma's lowercase words, the one split the api's content gate shares; `isUnitLemma(lemma)` is a lemma of more than one, which the gate's `isUnit` shares, and `isUnitLesson(lesson)` a lesson whose every word is one. `stageOf(curriculum, cards, placedOutBands, bands)` answers a `Stage`, the ids of the lessons from the first up to, not including, the first one neither studied (`holdsLessonCards`) nor placed out of (`isLessonPlacedOut`, which `nextStepOf` shares), every unit lesson left out first, so an unstudied one never cuts it. `recallFrameOf(word, card, stage)` answers the frame a recall shows: among the later frames whose `fromLessonId` the stage holds, the one with the lowest `mixed32` of the word's keyed hash XOR its position in `frames` (1 or 2), the anchor when the stage holds none; the keyed hash is `keyedTextHashOf(wordId, keyMilliseconds)` (`hash/`) over `keyInstantOf(card)` (`lastReviewAt`, else `enteredAt`), the same two `recognitionChoicesOf` orders its choices by, so the frame holds across reads of one stage and moves after a review. A reading and a recognition show `anchorFrameOf(word)`, the first of `frames`. `shownFrameOf(word, card, stage)` is the text a review shows: the gapped text of `recallFrameOf` for a recall, the anchor's full text for a recognition. The frame shown (#1007, ADR 044): `showDueReviews({ playerId, cards, stage }, { pendingReviews, lessons, clock, terms })` answers a `ShownReview` (`lesson`, `word`, `due`, a `DueCard`, and `shownFrame`) per card of `dueCardsAt` at the clock, in due order, and refuses `UnknownWord` for a due card no lesson holds, writing nothing. A due review keeps the frame it first showed: a pending review that holds a `shownFrame` answers it again, whatever the stage has become, and every other due card takes `shownFrameOf` at the given stage and stores it on its pending review, keeping a hint and a near-miss already there, all in one `saveAll`, none when nothing is new. The stage can grow between a read and its answer, so the answer never recomputes the frame: `answerReview` copies the pending review's. `taughtWordOf(wordId, lesson)` is the one lookup of a word in its lesson, a `TaughtWord` `{ lesson, word }` or `UnknownWord`, which `admitDueReview` shares, and `freshPendingReview` is the pending review with no hint, no near-miss and no frame that every write starts from when the card has none. `gradeTypedAnswer(answer, acceptedForms, isHeadword)` compares after NFC, ’ read as ', trim, inner spaces collapsed and lower case: `correct`, `nearMiss` or `wrong` (rule 4). Against a single-word form a near-miss is an answer at optimal-string-alignment distance 1 from a form of 4 letters or more, the answer not itself a headword. Against a multi-word unit (ADR 044, The card of a unit) it is either the unit's words, all equal but one, that one a near-miss of its counterpart by the single-word rule (so a slip in a particle of 2 or 3 letters is `wrong`), or the unit with one space dropped.
- `answerReview({ playerId, wordId, answer })` refuses, writing nothing, `UnknownWord` (no lesson holds the word), `WordNotHeld` (the player holds no card of it), then `ReviewNotDue { wordId, dueAt }` (`admitDueReview`, shared with `requestHint`). A recognition answer is a choice id, right exactly when `recognitionChoicesOf`, recomputed for the card at the answer, gives that id to the asked word, wrong when it gives it to another word; an id the card does not offer (a stale tab, the word id itself) is refused as `UnknownChoice { wordId }`, writing nothing, never rated Again. A recall near-miss with no near-miss pending answers `nearMiss` and stores it on the pending review, keeping its `shownFrame`, rating nothing; a second near-miss rates Again. The rating (`ratingOf`): wrong Again, right after a hint or a near-miss Hard, right otherwise Good, Easy never (rule 5). It steps the card through `reviewMemoryCard`, saves it, deletes the pending review, appends one `ReviewLogEntry` (player, word, instant, format, raw answer, the near-miss before it, `hintShown`, rating, elapsed whole days or null on a first review, `shownFrame`, the pending review's, null when no read showed the review a frame, the card before and after, rule 8), and credits no stock. When every word of the lesson the player holds, and at least one, then holds a stability of 7 or more and a `retainedAt` (`isLessonRetained`, over `isWordRetained` in its own file, which the overview reads per word, rule 7), it earns the skill: it reads `skills.skillsOf(playerId)` (`EarnedSkillRepository`, `town/`) and earns nothing when the lesson's skill is there; otherwise it reads the player's town (`towns.townOf`) before any save, saves the `TownSkill` (`lessonId`, the lesson's `resource` and `ratePercent`, `earnedAt` the review instant) through `skills.save`, and, when the player holds a town, settles it to the earn (`settleTownTo`), earns it there (`earnTownSkill`) and saves the town with every event (`saveChangedTown`: the settle's, then `skillEarned`); with no town it saves the skill alone. It is earned once and never taken back, and reading the town before the skill is saved keeps a store that loads the skills with the town from earning it twice. `skillLevelOf(skills, resource)` counts the skills on a resource, generic over the resource type. It answers `{ kind: 'nearMiss' }` or `{ kind: 'rated', rating, card, earnedSkill }`, `earnedSkill` a `TownSkill` or `undefined`. Its dependencies are `cards`, `pendingReviews`, `reviewLog`, `lessons`, `clock`, `terms`, `skills`, `towns`, `news` and `catalog` (a `TownCatalog`).
- `requestHint({ playerId, wordId })` refuses as `answerReview` does, then `HintOnRecognition` for a card in `learning`, and writes nothing then; otherwise it sets `hintShown` on the pending review, keeping a pending near-miss and the frame shown, and answers a `Hint`, `{ words }`: one `WordHint` `{ firstLetter, letterCount }` per word of the first accepted form, in order (rule 4, ADR 044).
- `grid/lowestFreeCell(taken, cellsPerRow)` (#759) is the lowest-free placement: rows from 1, cells from 1 within a row, the first `Cell` (`{ row, column }`) not taken, `InvalidCellsPerRow` below 1 or fractional. `foundTown` passes region 1's sites as area and site.
- `town/` (#759, ADR 040) is the town; it imports `season/` and `grid/`. `Town` holds `id`, `playerId`, a `TownName` (trimmed, `BlankTownName`), a `TownAddress` (`region`, `area`, `site`, whole from 1, `InvalidTownAddress`), four `Stocks` (`money`, `food`, `power`, `material`), `storedAt`, `fullSince` and the seven `BuildingLevels` (`homes`, `farm`, `factory`, `powerStation`, `storage`, `school`, `office`); `Town.found` starts homes and the school at level 1 with the content's `startingStocks` and `noStoreFull`; `Town.restore` refuses a stock that is not whole from 0 (`InvalidTownStock`) and a level that is not (`InvalidTownBuildingLevel`). Its terrain is `terrainOf(area)`: fields, river, hills in rotation. `TownCatalog` (`levelOf`, `townSettings`) answers a `BuildingLevel` union (homes `peopleSupplied`, the farm, factory and power station `ratePerHour`, storage `capacity`, the school `lessonPercent` and `learningCap`, the office `hires`) and the `TownSettings` (`taxRatePerHour`, `basePeople`, `cityPeople`, `storageBaseCapacity`, `sitesPerArea`, `terrainBonusPerHour`, `seasons` with a `productionPercent` per season and resource). `deriveResourceRates` sums tax for money, the farm for food, the factory for material, the power station for power, adds `terrainBonusPerHour` to the terrain's resource (fields food, river power, hills material), then scales each by the season's percent in one division; `accrueTown(town, catalog, to)` accrues per season segment, each store through `storeAfterSegment`, up to `deriveStorageCapacity` (`storageBaseCapacity` at level 0) and answers an `Accrual`, the `stocks` and the `fullSince` each store holds at `to` (a store already full keeps its stored instant, a store that crosses the capacity takes the crossing, `from + ceil((capacity − amount) × 3_600_000 / rate)`); `deriveFullAt(town, catalog)` (#1021, ADR 027) answers a `FullAt`, per store the instant it reaches the capacity from the town as stored: the stored `fullSince` (else `storedAt`) for a store already full, the crossing walking forward from `storedAt` through the same steps, or `null` once `SEASONS_PER_YEAR + 1` segments in a row leave the stock unchanged, a whole year of seasons after any segment before the epoch, so a rate of 0 in every season answers `null`; both walks take each step from `storesAfterSeasonSegment` (one season segment, up to `to` when given, every store through `storeAfterSegment` at the rates of its start) over `storedStoresOf`, so they cannot disagree; `rebaseFullSince(accrued, after, catalog)` keeps that instant for each store `after` holds at or above the capacity and clears the rest, after every mutation and every finish; `derivePeople` answers `supplied` (base plus the homes' `peopleSupplied`), `occupied` (every built level's `people` plus each kind's hires times its `people`) and `free`; `townLabelOf` answers `city` once `supplied` reaches `cityPeople`. A missing level answers `UnknownTownBuildingLevel`. `TownCatalog.hireTermsOf(hire)` answers a kind's `HireTerms` (`cost`, `durationSeconds`, `people`, `strength`, `carry`, `roadPercent`). `foundTown({ playerId, name }, { towns, catalog, clock, ids, skills })` refuses `BlankTownName`, then `PlayerAlreadyHoldsTown`, places at `lowestFreeCell` over region 1 with the player's earned skills (`skills.skillsOf`), and answers what `TownRepository.save` refuses (`SiteTaken`, `PlayerAlreadyHoldsTown`) on a race. `TownRepository` (`holdsTown`, `occupiedSites`, `townOf(playerId)`, `save`) is in `town/`; tests pass `testing/inMemoryTowns.ts` and `testing/townCatalogOf.ts`. `deriveResourceRates(buildingLevels, terrain, skills, catalog, at)` scales a resource holding skills by one `100 + Σ ratePercent` before the season's percent, `rate × product / 100 ** count` (ADR 039, rule 4).
- `season/` holds the calendar the town reads: `seasonAt(instant, settings)` answers a `Season` (`kind`, `year` from 1, `endsAt`) or `undefined` before the epoch; `SeasonedSettings` is `{ seasons: SeasonDates }` and `SeasonDates` is `{ epoch, daysPerSeason }`, the least the arithmetic reads, so `TownSettings` satisfies it; `nextSeasonBoundaryAfter(instant, settings)` answers the season's `endsAt`, or the epoch before it; `deriveBuildDurationSeconds(durationSeconds, buildPercent)` is `ceil(durationSeconds × buildPercent / 100)`, the one rounding of a build duration.
- The shared projection fixture is `town/projection-cases.json` (ADR 034, #577): `town/projectionCases.test.ts` feeds each case to `storeAfterSegment(store, ratePerHour, capacity, from, to)`, the per-store accrual `accrueTown` runs for every segment, and Android's `SharedProjectionCasesTest` reads the same file. A case either side cannot meet fails its test; a new projection rule is a new case there first.
- The school (#768, #769, ADR 040): `Town` carries `skills`, a `TownSkill` per earn (`lessonId`, the town's `resource`, `ratePercent`, `earnedAt`) that `accrueTown` reads; `StoredTown` and `TownFounding` carry them, so `Town.restore` and `Town.found` keep the stored skills. The player's skills live behind `EarnedSkillRepository` (`skillsOf(playerId)`, `save(playerId, skill)`); tests pass `testing/inMemoryEarnedSkills.ts`. `earnTownSkill(town, skill, catalog)` accrues to `earnedAt` at the skills before it, appends the skill, re-bases and answers `skillEarned`. The lesson slot (`LessonSlot`, idle or busy with `lessonId`, `startedAt`, `finishesAt`) is behind `LessonSlotRepository` (`lessonSlotOf(townId)`, idle when none is stored, and `save`); tests pass `testing/inMemoryLessonSlots.ts`. `settledLessonSlot(slot, held, starts, lessons, now)` applies a finished lesson in memory: its cards entered at `finishesAt` unless held, leaving out every word held out at `finishesAt` (`heldOutWordsAt(starts, finishesAt)`, so a lesson finished before a release but settled after it still withholds the band's words) and every unit any of whose words is (`wordsEnteringOf`, ADR 044), the slot idle, `lessonStudied`; a finished lesson neither the catalog nor the player's released lessons hold settles as idle with no card and no event. `lessons` is a `SchoolLessons` (`schoolLessonsOf(catalog, released)`: the catalog's lesson, else the released one), and a `SchoolLesson` is a `Lesson` without its `skill`. `settledSchoolOf` reads the cards, the slot and the band starts and settles them, answering the `released` lessons over the settled cards; its sources add `lessonOfWord` and `bands.wordsOfBand`; `saveLessonSlot` saves the entered cards, the slot, then the events; `saveResolvedTown` saves a resolved town and its news only when the resolve finished work. `startSchoolLesson({ playerId, lessonId }, { towns, lessonSlots, cards, bandStarts, bands, placements, random, lessons, news, catalog, clock })` resolves the town, settles the slot, then refuses in order `TownNotFound`, `LessonNotFound` (neither the catalog nor the player's released lessons hold it), `LessonAlreadyStudied`, `LessonUnderStudy` (the slot studies it), `StudySlotBusy`, `LessonSchoolTooLow { requiredSchoolLevel, schoolLevel }` (the built school below the lesson's `requiredSchoolLevel`), then `LearningCapReached { learningCap, cardsInLearning, lessonWordCount }` when the cards in learning (`cardsInLearningOf`: stability null or under 7 days, exported from the root so the api's overview counts the same cards, #770) plus the lesson's words the player will hold pass the school level's `learningCap`. A refusal writes nothing; a start costs nothing, saves the resolved town and its finished work's news when the resolve changed it, then the entered cards, the busy slot and the finish's events. The duration is `ceil(durationSeconds × lessonPercent × studyPercent / 10_000)` (`deriveLessonDurationSeconds`), the study percent from `studyPercentAt(now, settings)`, the season's `TownSettings.seasons.durationPercent[season].study` (`TownDurationPercent` is `{ build, study }`), 100 before the epoch. `stopSchoolLesson({ playerId, lessonId }, { towns, lessonSlots, cards, bandStarts, lessons, bands, news, catalog, clock })` resolves the town and settles the slot, then idles a slot busy with that lesson and records `lessonStopped` at `now`; a lesson finished by `now` or not in the slot is refused `LessonStudyNotFound`, writing nothing. `settleSchoolLesson(playerId, { towns, lessonSlots, cards, bandStarts, lessons, bands, news, clock })` does nothing for a player with no town or a slot it leaves as stored; otherwise it saves the entered cards, the idle slot and `lessonStudied`, so the api calls it before every learning read (ADR 005).
- The holdout's draw (#744, ADR 037): a `BandStart` (`band`, `startedAt`, `heldOutWords`, `checks`) is stored once per player and band behind `BandStartRepository` (`bandStartsOf(playerId)`, `save`, which keeps the first draw, and `saveCheck(playerId, band, check)`, which replaces that band's check of the same week); tests pass `testing/inMemoryBandStarts.ts`. `heldOutWords` are `Headword`s, the lowercase lemma (`headwordOf`, the one rule the api's catalog shares, since the list holds `TRUE` and `FALSE`), never a `WordId`: the draw spans the whole band, and a later content batch gives most of its words their lexicon entry and id after the draw. `BandCatalog` (`bandOfLesson(lessonId)`, `undefined` for a chrome lesson, `wordsOfBand(band)`, each a `BandWord` `{ headword, isChrome }`, and `hasLessons(band)`) comes from the content; tests pass `testing/inMemoryBands.ts`. `startSchoolLesson` draws when a lesson of a band the player has not started and did not place out of (`placedOutBandsOf` over the stored `PlacementRepository` scores) starts: `bandStartDrawn` takes ⌊10 %⌋ of the band's words outside the chrome lessons, drawn among those no card the player holds practises (`practisedWordsOf`, every word of every held card's lemma, so a unit studied ahead of the band keeps its words out of the draw, ADR 044), through the `RandomSource` port, the only randomness a use case reads; `sampleOf(items, count)` takes a count no larger than `items`, so every caller caps it, and `testing/firstItemsRandom.ts` throws on a larger one as the api's adapter does. A refused start stores no draw; a stopped lesson keeps it. A lesson whose every word is held out enters no card, so it counts as unstudied: its words are in neither check nor the release, and enter when the player studies it after the release (ADR 037).
- The holdout's checks and the release (#804, ADR 037, ADR 041, ADR 044), in `holdout/`: a `HoldoutCheck` of week 8 or 24 (`HoldoutCheckWeek`) is `begun` (`begunAt`, a `HoldoutCheckSample` `{ wordIds, heldOutWordIds }`, `wordIds` in the asked order), `taken` (the begun fields, `heldOutScore`, `practisedScore`, `takenAt`) or `skipped` (`skippedAt`, no sample). Due on read, never by a timer (`isHoldoutCheckDue`, `holdoutCheckWeeks.ts`): from 8 or 24 whole weeks after the band's `startedAt` until it is taken or skipped; the 8-week check lapses once the 24-week one falls due, since taken then it would measure 24 weeks. `readHoldoutChecks({ playerId }, { bandStarts, words, clock })` answers a `HoldoutCheckView` per due check, `due` or, once begun, `asking` with its `PlacementItem`s (`placementItemsOf`, the placement's format and choices, shared with `placementViewOf`). `beginHoldoutCheck({ playerId, band, week }, { bandStarts, cards, lessons, words, random, clock })` refuses `HoldoutCheckNotDue { band, week }`, answers a begun check's stored words again, and otherwise draws `holdoutCheckSampleDrawn`: among the band's placement words outside the chrome lessons whose lesson the player holds a card of (`holdsLessonCards`, the one test of a studied lesson), every held-out one and, through the `RandomSource`, as many of the held ones, or every held one when fewer are held, then the whole sample in a random order. `answerHoldoutCheck({ playerId, band, week, answers })` refuses `HoldoutCheckNotDue`, then `HoldoutCheckNotBegun`, then `InvalidHoldoutCheckAnswers` unless one offered choice answers each asked word (`rightWordIdsOf`, shared with `answerPlacement`), and stores the held-out and the practised right answers apart. `skipHoldoutCheck` refuses `HoldoutCheckNotDue` and stores the skip, dropping a begun sample. `releasedAtOf(start)` is the 24-week check's `takenAt` or `skippedAt`; `heldOutWordsAt(starts, at)` is every band's held-out words not released at `at`, the one test every held-out reader shares. `releasedLessonsOf(starts, held, { lessons, bands })` answers each released band's lessons: the words of every lesson the player held a card of entered before the release whose lemma holds one of the band's held-out words, in rank order (`wordsOfBand`), 5 words to a lesson, a unit riding in the lesson of its last-ranked held-out word and entered with the band that releases last of those holding its words, each `released-<band>-<n>` with the highest duration and school level of its words' lessons. A word of a lesson studied after the release enters with its own lesson. `answerReview` earns no skill on the review of a released word and leaves the released words out of `isLessonRetained`, so a release neither earns nor holds back a skill; its dependencies add `bandStarts` and `bands`.
- Placement (#743, ADR 037, ADR 041), in `placement/`: a `Placement` is `skipped` (`skippedAt`) or `taken` (`begunAt`, `samples`, one `PlacementSample` `{ band, wordIds }` per band in band order, and `scores`, a `PlacementScore` `{ band, score, takenAt }` per band answered), behind `PlacementRepository` (`placementOf(playerId)`, `save`, which keeps the first placement and each band's first score); tests pass `testing/inMemoryPlacements.ts`. `PlacementWordCatalog` (`placementWordsOf(band)`, each a `PlacementWord` `{ id, headword, word, gloss, isChrome }`) comes from the content; tests pass `testing/placementWords.ts`, whose `placementContentOf(shelves)` answers both the words and the `BandCatalog` side. A band is placeable (`placementSamplesDrawn.ts`) when it is drafted, every headword of `BandCatalog.wordsOfBand(band)` holding a placement word, when the next band, if it has words, has at least one lesson (`BandCatalog.hasLessons`), since a player placed out of it starts there (ADR 037), and the last band, with no words after it, is placed out on its score alone; one lesson is enough, since ADR 041 holds placement back for the band's lexicon entries, which the drafted test already demands, not for a fully drafted next band, and when it holds 30 words outside the chrome lessons. A placement is offered (`isPlacementOffered`) while the player has started no band and band 1 is placeable (ADR 041: placement waits until band 1's lessons are drafted). `beginPlacement({ playerId }, { placements, bandStarts, words, bands, random, clock })` refuses `PlacementAlreadyTaken` (stored, taken or skipped), then `PlacementNotOffered`, and stores 30 words per band through the `RandomSource`, from band 1 up while each band is placeable (`placementSamplesDrawn`). `skipPlacement` refuses the same and stores the skip. `readPlacement` answers a `PlacementView`: `offered`, `asking` (the band, its `PlacementItem`s `{ wordId, word, choices }` and the scores), or `closed` (the scores). The asked band (`askedSampleOf`) is the first sample with no score, none once a score falls under 29 (the check stops at the first band failed) or once that band has started. Each item offers its word's gloss and up to 3 others of the band's sample, no two choices sharing a gloss, chosen and ordered by `textHashOf` (`hash/`) seeded by the asked word's id, each `{ choiceId, gloss }` with ids from `choiceIdsOf` (`hash/`, shared with `recognitionChoicesOf`), opaque and deterministic, never a word id. `answerPlacement({ playerId, band, answers })` refuses `PlacementBandNotAsked { band }`, then `InvalidPlacementAnswers { band }` unless the answers hold exactly one offered choice per asked word, and stores the score, the right answers' count, at `clock.now()`. `placedOutBandsOf(scores)` answers the bands scored 29 or more; `placementScoresOf(placement)` answers a taken placement's scores and none otherwise. `nextStepOf` takes `placedOutBands` in its reading and `bands` in its terms and passes over every lesson of a placed-out band and nothing else, so the next lesson is the first one the player has neither studied nor placed out of: an unstudied chrome lesson, never placed out, still comes first, and otherwise the first lesson of the first band failed; a placed-out lesson stays startable, draws no holdout and earns its skill as any lesson does.
