# packages/domain

The game rules as pure TypeScript (ADR 004). Everything else depends on this package; it depends on nothing.

## Commands

- `pnpm --filter @mygame/domain typecheck` — `tsc --noEmit` over `src` and the Vitest config.
- `pnpm --filter @mygame/domain test` — Vitest, runs `src/**/*.test.ts`.
- `pnpm gate` from the repo root before every commit.

## Entry

`src/index.ts` is the single entry (`exports["."]`); the town's names sit beside the others, its rules under `src/town/`. Consumers import from `@mygame/domain`, never from a path under `src`.

## Gotchas

- Zero runtime dependencies. `typescript` and `vitest` are dev dependencies taken from the workspace `catalog:`; `jq '.dependencies' package.json` prints `null`.
- No `node:` imports, no framework, no `zod`. The leak check in `.claude/rules/architecture.md` must stay empty.
- No comments of any kind, including JSDoc. The narrow exceptions are in `.claude/rules/typescript-style.md`.
- Explicit return type on every exported function, method and exported arrow.
- Nothing throws. A failure is a member of the `DomainError` union returned inside a `Result`; `rg -n "extends Error|throw new" src` stays empty, test files included. In a test, narrow a `Result` with Vitest's `assert(result.ok)` instead of throwing.
- Time comes only from the `Clock` port. `Date.now()`, `new Date()`, `setTimeout` and `setInterval` never appear in `src`, tests included; a test builds a `frozenClock` beside itself.
- `Instant` is stored as epoch milliseconds; `Duration` is whole or fractional seconds and never negative.
- Imports are extensionless (`'./Duration'`); the base tsconfig resolves as `Bundler` without `allowImportingTsExtensions`.
- `memory/` is the FSRS-6 scheduler of ADR 036, a port of py-fsrs 6.3.2 at commit `9446cb06` with no fuzz: no port, no use case, no content; `learning/` and the learning use cases put it in the game (below). `MemoryCard.entered(enteredAt)` enters a card in `learning` at step 0 with `stability`, `difficulty`, `lastReviewAt` and `lastRating` null; `MemoryCard.restore` refuses a step that is not a whole count from 0, a stability not above 0 or a difficulty outside 1 to 10 (`InvalidMemoryCard`). `reviewMemoryCard(card, rating, at, terms)` answers the next card and refuses `ReviewBeforeLastReview`; it never checks the due instant, so a use case refuses a card not yet due. `retrievabilityOf(card, at, terms)` and `dueInstantOf(card, terms)` derive on read, nothing stored; `dueInstantOf` refuses `UnknownMemoryStep` for a learning or relearning step the terms do not hold. Whole days are floored (`wholeDaysBetween`), a review interval is rounded half to even as Python's `round` (2.5 days is 2, 3.5 is 4), and every `e ** x` is `Math.E ** x`, never `Math.exp`, as py-fsrs writes `math.e ** x`. `retainedAt` is the review instant of a Good from `review` or `relearning` (a recall) at least 7 whole days after the review before it, null after any other review. `defaultMemoryTerms` holds py-fsrs's defaults.
- `learning/` holds the lesson rules of ADR 036 (#711). A `Lesson` (`id`, `durationSeconds`, `requiredSchoolLevel`, `words`, `skill`, a `LessonSkill` `{ resource, ratePercent }` on the town's `ResourceKind`) holds `LessonWord`s (`id`, `acceptedForms`, a non-empty tuple, `gloss`, `frame`, `gappedFrame`, the frame with the word blanked, which a recall shows); `LessonCatalog` (`ports/LessonCatalog.ts`) answers `lessonOf(lessonId)`, `lessonOfWord(wordId)` and `isHeadword(word)` (the NGSL 1.2 list). Cards are the player's, never the town's (rule 1): `CardRepository` (`cardsOf(playerId)`, `save(playerId, held)`, a `HeldCard` `{ wordId, card }`), `PendingReviewRepository` (`pendingReviewOf`, `save`, `delete`, keyed by player and word, a `PendingReview` `{ hintShown, nearMissAnswer }`) and `ReviewLog` (`append(entry)`, append-only, never read by the domain). Tests pass `inMemoryCards`, `inMemoryPendingReviews`, `inMemoryReviewLog`, `inMemoryLessons` and `testing/lessonOne.ts`.
- `dueCardsAt(cards, now, terms)` answers the cards whose `dueInstantOf` is at or before `now`, in due order, a tie by word id. `reviewFormatOf(card)` is `recognition` in `learning`, `recall` otherwise (rule 2). `recognitionChoicesOf(wordId, lesson, card, held)` answers the word and at most 4 others of its lesson the player holds a card for, so a held-out gloss is never offered, the 4 with the lowest hash, then all ordered by hash, a tie by word id; the hash mixes each word id with the card's key instant (`lastReviewAt`, else `enteredAt`) through `mixed32` (`hash/mixed32.ts`), so the order holds across reads and moves after each review (rule 3). Each `RecognitionChoice` is `{ choiceId, wordId }`: the choice id is the 8 hex digits of the word's hash, re-mixed through `mixed32` until no earlier choice of the card holds it, so it is unique within the card, never a word id, and changes with the key instant; the api sends only the choice id and the gloss. The ids are opaque and deterministic, not secret: they guard against a client bug leaking the answer, not against a modified client (ADR 036 rule 3). `gradeTypedAnswer(answer, acceptedForms, isHeadword)` compares after NFC, ’ read as ', trim, inner spaces collapsed and lower case: `correct`, `nearMiss` (optimal-string-alignment distance 1 from a form of 4 letters or more, the answer not itself a headword) or `wrong` (rule 4).
- `answerReview({ playerId, wordId, answer })` refuses, writing nothing, `UnknownWord` (no lesson holds the word), `WordNotHeld` (the player holds no card of it), then `ReviewNotDue { wordId, dueAt }` (`admitDueReview`, shared with `requestHint`). A recognition answer is a choice id, right exactly when `recognitionChoicesOf`, recomputed for the card at the answer, gives that id to the asked word, wrong when it gives it to another word; an id the card does not offer (a stale tab, the word id itself) is refused as `UnknownChoice { wordId }`, writing nothing, never rated Again. A recall near-miss with no near-miss pending answers `nearMiss` and stores it on the pending review, rating nothing; a second near-miss rates Again. The rating (`ratingOf`): wrong Again, right after a hint or a near-miss Hard, right otherwise Good, Easy never (rule 5). It steps the card through `reviewMemoryCard`, saves it, deletes the pending review, appends one `ReviewLogEntry` (player, word, instant, format, raw answer, the near-miss before it, `hintShown`, rating, elapsed whole days or null on a first review, the card before and after, rule 8), and credits no stock. When every word of the lesson the player holds, and at least one, then holds a stability of 7 or more and a `retainedAt` (`isLessonRetained`, over `isWordRetained` in its own file, which the overview reads per word, rule 7), it earns the skill: it reads `skills.skillsOf(playerId)` (`EarnedSkillRepository`, `town/`) and earns nothing when the lesson's skill is there; otherwise it reads the player's town (`towns.townOf`) before any save, saves the `TownSkill` (`lessonId`, the lesson's `resource` and `ratePercent`, `earnedAt` the review instant) through `skills.save`, and, when the player holds a town, settles it to the earn (`settleTownTo`), earns it there (`earnTownSkill`) and saves the town with every event (`saveChangedTown`: the settle's, then `skillEarned`); with no town it saves the skill alone. It is earned once and never taken back, and reading the town before the skill is saved keeps a store that loads the skills with the town from earning it twice. `skillLevelOf(skills, resource)` counts the skills on a resource, generic over the resource type. It answers `{ kind: 'nearMiss' }` or `{ kind: 'rated', rating, card, earnedSkill }`, `earnedSkill` a `TownSkill` or `undefined`. Its dependencies are `cards`, `pendingReviews`, `reviewLog`, `lessons`, `clock`, `terms`, `skills`, `towns`, `news` and `catalog` (a `TownCatalog`).
- `requestHint({ playerId, wordId })` refuses as `answerReview` does, then `HintOnRecognition` for a card in `learning`, and writes nothing then; otherwise it sets `hintShown` on the pending review, keeping a pending near-miss, and answers the first accepted form's first letter and letter count (rule 4).
- `grid/lowestFreeCell(taken, cellsPerRow)` (#759) is the lowest-free placement: rows from 1, cells from 1 within a row, the first `Cell` (`{ row, column }`) not taken, `InvalidCellsPerRow` below 1 or fractional. `foundTown` passes region 1's sites as area and site.
- `town/` (#759, ADR 040) is the town; it imports `season/` and `grid/`. `Town` holds `id`, `playerId`, a `TownName` (trimmed, `BlankTownName`), a `TownAddress` (`region`, `area`, `site`, whole from 1, `InvalidTownAddress`), four `Stocks` (`money`, `food`, `power`, `material`), `storedAt`, `fullSince` and the seven `BuildingLevels` (`homes`, `farm`, `factory`, `powerStation`, `storage`, `school`, `office`); `Town.found` starts homes and the school at level 1 with the content's `startingStocks` and `noStoreFull`; `Town.restore` refuses a stock that is not whole from 0 (`InvalidTownStock`) and a level that is not (`InvalidTownBuildingLevel`). Its terrain is `terrainOf(area)`: fields, river, hills in rotation. `TownCatalog` (`levelOf`, `townSettings`) answers a `BuildingLevel` union (homes `peopleSupplied`, the farm, factory and power station `ratePerHour`, storage `capacity`, the school `lessonPercent` and `learningCap`, the office `hires`) and the `TownSettings` (`taxRatePerHour`, `basePeople`, `cityPeople`, `storageBaseCapacity`, `sitesPerArea`, `terrainBonusPerHour`, `seasons` with a `productionPercent` per season and resource). `deriveResourceRates` sums tax for money, the farm for food, the factory for material, the power station for power, adds `terrainBonusPerHour` to the terrain's resource (fields food, river power, hills material), then scales each by the season's percent in one division; `accrueTown(town, catalog, to)` accrues per season segment, each store through `storeAfterSegment`, up to `deriveStorageCapacity` (`storageBaseCapacity` at level 0) and answers an `Accrual`, the `stocks` and the `fullSince` each store holds at `to` (a store already full keeps its stored instant, a store that crosses the capacity takes the crossing, `from + ceil((capacity − amount) × 3_600_000 / rate)`); `rebaseFullSince(accrued, after, catalog)` keeps that instant for each store `after` holds at or above the capacity and clears the rest, after every mutation and every finish; `derivePeople` answers `supplied` (base plus the homes' `peopleSupplied`), `occupied` (every built level's `people` plus each kind's hires times its `people`) and `free`; `townLabelOf` answers `city` once `supplied` reaches `cityPeople`. A missing level answers `UnknownTownBuildingLevel`. `TownCatalog.hireTermsOf(hire)` answers a kind's `HireTerms` (`cost`, `durationSeconds`, `people`, `strength`, `carry`, `roadPercent`). `foundTown({ playerId, name }, { towns, catalog, clock, ids, skills })` refuses `BlankTownName`, then `PlayerAlreadyHoldsTown`, places at `lowestFreeCell` over region 1 with the player's earned skills (`skills.skillsOf`), and answers what `TownRepository.save` refuses (`SiteTaken`, `PlayerAlreadyHoldsTown`) on a race. `TownRepository` (`holdsTown`, `occupiedSites`, `townOf(playerId)`, `save`) is in `town/`; tests pass `testing/inMemoryTowns.ts` and `testing/townCatalogOf.ts`. `deriveResourceRates(buildingLevels, terrain, skills, catalog, at)` scales a resource holding skills by one `100 + Σ ratePercent` before the season's percent, `rate × product / 100 ** count` (ADR 039, rule 4).
- `season/` holds the calendar the town reads: `seasonAt(instant, settings)` answers a `Season` (`kind`, `year` from 1, `endsAt`) or `undefined` before the epoch; `SeasonedSettings` is `{ seasons: SeasonDates }` and `SeasonDates` is `{ epoch, daysPerSeason }`, the least the arithmetic reads, so `TownSettings` satisfies it; `nextSeasonBoundaryAfter(instant, settings)` answers the season's `endsAt`, or the epoch before it; `deriveBuildDurationSeconds(durationSeconds, buildPercent)` is `ceil(durationSeconds × buildPercent / 100)`, the one rounding of a build duration.
- The shared projection fixture is `town/projection-cases.json` (ADR 034, #577): `town/projectionCases.test.ts` feeds each case to `storeAfterSegment(store, ratePerHour, capacity, from, to)`, the per-store accrual `accrueTown` runs for every segment, and Android's `SharedProjectionCasesTest` reads the same file. A case either side cannot meet fails its test; a new projection rule is a new case there first.
- The school (#768, #769, ADR 040): `Town` carries `skills`, a `TownSkill` per earn (`lessonId`, the town's `resource`, `ratePercent`, `earnedAt`) that `accrueTown` reads; `StoredTown` and `TownFounding` carry them, so `Town.restore` and `Town.found` keep the stored skills. The player's skills live behind `EarnedSkillRepository` (`skillsOf(playerId)`, `save(playerId, skill)`); tests pass `testing/inMemoryEarnedSkills.ts`. `earnTownSkill(town, skill, catalog)` accrues to `earnedAt` at the skills before it, appends the skill, re-bases and answers `skillEarned`. The lesson slot (`LessonSlot`, idle or busy with `lessonId`, `startedAt`, `finishesAt`) is behind `LessonSlotRepository` (`lessonSlotOf(townId)`, idle when none is stored, and `save`); tests pass `testing/inMemoryLessonSlots.ts`. `settledLessonSlot(slot, held, heldOut, lessons, now)` applies a finished lesson in memory: its cards entered at `finishesAt` unless held, leaving out every held-out word (`wordsEnteringOf`), the slot idle, `lessonStudied`; a finished lesson gone from the catalog settles as idle with no card and no event. `settledSchoolOf` reads the cards, the slot and the band starts and settles them; `saveLessonSlot` saves the entered cards, the slot, then the events; `saveResolvedTown` saves a resolved town and its news only when the resolve finished work. `startSchoolLesson({ playerId, lessonId }, { towns, lessonSlots, cards, bandStarts, bands, placedOutBands, random, lessons, news, catalog, clock })` resolves the town, settles the slot, then refuses in order `TownNotFound`, `LessonNotFound`, `LessonAlreadyStudied`, `LessonUnderStudy` (the slot studies it), `StudySlotBusy`, `LessonSchoolTooLow { requiredSchoolLevel, schoolLevel }` (the built school below the lesson's `requiredSchoolLevel`), then `LearningCapReached { learningCap, cardsInLearning, lessonWordCount }` when the cards in learning (`cardsInLearningOf`: stability null or under 7 days, exported from the root so the api's overview counts the same cards, #770) plus the lesson's words the player will hold pass the school level's `learningCap`. A refusal writes nothing; a start costs nothing, saves the resolved town and its finished work's news when the resolve changed it, then the entered cards, the busy slot and the finish's events. The duration is `ceil(durationSeconds × lessonPercent × studyPercent / 10_000)` (`deriveLessonDurationSeconds`), the study percent from `studyPercentAt(now, settings)`, the season's `TownSettings.seasons.durationPercent[season].study` (`TownDurationPercent` is `{ build, study }`), 100 before the epoch. `stopSchoolLesson({ playerId, lessonId }, { towns, lessonSlots, cards, bandStarts, lessons, news, catalog, clock })` resolves the town and settles the slot, then idles a slot busy with that lesson and records `lessonStopped` at `now`; a lesson finished by `now` or not in the slot is refused `LessonStudyNotFound`, writing nothing. `settleSchoolLesson(playerId, { towns, lessonSlots, cards, bandStarts, lessons, news, clock })` does nothing for a player with no town or a slot it leaves as stored; otherwise it saves the entered cards, the idle slot and `lessonStudied`, so the api calls it before every learning read (ADR 005).
- The holdout's draw (#744, ADR 037): a `BandStart` (`band`, `startedAt`, `heldOutWords`) is stored once per player and band behind `BandStartRepository` (`bandStartsOf(playerId)`, `save`, which keeps the first draw); tests pass `testing/inMemoryBandStarts.ts`. `heldOutWords` are `Headword`s, the lowercase lemma (`headwordOf`, the one rule the api's catalog shares, since the list holds `TRUE` and `FALSE`), never a `WordId`: the draw spans the whole band, and a later content batch gives most of its words their lexicon entry and id after the draw. `BandCatalog` (`bandOfLesson(lessonId)`, `undefined` for a chrome lesson, and `wordsOfBand(band)`, each a `BandWord` `{ headword, isChrome }`) comes from the content; tests pass `testing/inMemoryBands.ts`. `startSchoolLesson` draws when a lesson of a band the player has not started and did not place out of (`PlacedOutBands`) starts: `bandStartDrawn` takes ⌊10 %⌋ of the band's words outside the chrome lessons through the `RandomSource` port, the only randomness a use case reads (`testing/firstItemsRandom.ts`). A refused start stores no draw; a stopped lesson keeps it. A lesson whose every word is held out enters no card, so nothing marks it studied until its words are released (#804).
- Building work (#764, ADR 040, ADR 011, ADR 017): `Town` carries a `WorkSlot` (`idle`, or `busy` with `building`, `level`, the debited `cost`, `startedAt`, `finishesAt`) and a `WaitingList` of `WaitingWork` (`building`, `level`, `cost`, `durationSeconds`), all fixed at the start, so a later content change never touches stored work. `Town.restore` refuses a slot that starts after it finishes (`SlotStartsAfterFinish`) or finishes before `storedAt` (`SlotFinishesBeforeStored`), a level below 1 (`InvalidTownBuildingLevel`), a negative cost (`NegativeResourceAmount`), a fractional cost (`FractionalResourceAmount`), and a waiting duration that is negative or fractional. `startBuildingWork({ playerId, building }, { towns, news, catalog, clock })` resolves the town, then refuses in order `TownNotFound`, `WaitingListFull { cap }` (`TownSettings.waitingListCap` entries behind a busy slot), `TopLevelReached` or `UnknownTownBuildingLevel` (the next level after `Town.projectedLevels`, the built levels with the slot and every waiting entry applied), `NotEnoughPeople { requiredPeople, freePeople }` (the free people of the projection with the new level below 0), then `NotEnoughResources { missing }`; it debits the cost at `now`, fixes `durationSeconds` with `deriveBuildDurationSeconds` (moved to `season/`) at `buildPercentAt(now, settings)` (`TownSettings.seasons.durationPercent`, 100 before the epoch), and starts the work in an idle slot or appends it. `stopBuildingWork({ playerId, building, level }, deps)` follows ADR 011: it refunds the full stored cost of the named entry (`BuildingWorkNotFound` when neither the slot nor the waiting list holds it), then `recheckWaitingList` rechecks the remaining waiting entries in order against the projection (the built levels plus the slot still busy plus every entry kept before it): an entry whose level is not the projected level plus one, or whose people would pass the free people, is stopped in cascade with its own full refund. Stopping the slot's work starts the first kept entry at the stop instant. Every stopped entry answers one `buildingWorkStopped` at the stop instant, the named one first, then the cascade in order. `finishBuildingWork` applies every finished slot in order on read: it accrues to `finishesAt`, applies the level, starts the next waiting entry at that instant and re-bases the stocks. `resolveTown` saves only when a finish applied. Every change answers a `ChangedTown` (`{ town, events }`) and `saveChangedTown` saves the town, then calls `TownNews.record(townId, events)`, so the events commit with the change that wrote them: `buildingWorkFinished` at each `finishesAt`, `becameCity` when a finish makes `townLabelOf` turn `city`, `buildingWorkStopped` per stopped entry at the stop instant. `TownEvent` (`town/TownEvent.ts`) covers every kind of ADR 040's Town News list; nothing in the domain reads it back. Tests pass `testing/inMemoryTownNews.ts` (`recordedEventsOf(townId)`).
- Hiring (#777, ADR 040, ADR 018, ADR 021): `Town` carries `hires` (`Hires`, a count per `HireKind`, `worker` and `truck`) and a `HiringSlot` (`idle`, or a busy `HiringOrder` with `hire`, `count`, `costPerHire`, `secondsPerHire`, `startedAt`, all fixed at the order). `Town.restore` refuses a stored count that is not whole from 0 and an order count that is not whole from 1 (`InvalidHireCount`), seconds per hire that are not whole from 1 (`InvalidHireDuration`) and an invalid cost. `town.hiresAt(at)` answers the stored hires plus `deliveredHiresOf(order, at)`, `min(count, floor(elapsed / secondsPerHire))`, so hires arrive one by one on read; `town.staffedHires` counts the whole order from the instant it is placed, and every `derivePeople` call that judges people passes it. `placeHiringOrder({ playerId, hire, count }, { towns, news, catalog, clock })` resolves the town, then refuses in order `InvalidHireCount`, `OfficeTooLow { hire, requiredOfficeLevel, officeLevel }` (the built office level's `hires` lacks the kind; the required level is the lowest that lists it), `HiringSlotBusy`, `NotEnoughPeople` (`count × people` above `lowestFreePeopleOf`, the lowest free count across the built levels and every step of the work slot and the waiting list), then `NotEnoughResources`; it debits `count × cost` at `now` and fixes `secondsPerHire` with `deriveHireDurationSeconds`, `ceil(durationSeconds × hirePercent / (100 × (1 + officeLevel)))`, the built office level and `hirePercentAt(now, settings)` (`TownDurationPercent.hire`, 100 before the epoch). `resolvedTownOf` closes an order whose last hire is delivered (`Town.closeHiring`): the count moves into `hires`, the slot goes idle, `storedAt` and the stocks stay, and `hiringDelivered` is stamped with the order's end. `stopHiringOrder({ playerId, hire, startedAt }, deps)` keeps the hires delivered by `now`, refunds `costPerHire × stopped` in full and answers `hiringStopped` with the delivered and stopped counts; an order of another kind or start, or one already closed, is refused `HiringOrderNotFound`.
- Jobs and the region map (#781, ADR 040, ADR 014, ADR 019, ADR 022): `readRegionMap({ playerId, area? }, { map, catalog })` reads one area of the viewer's region through `RegionMapReader` (`town/RegionMapReader.ts`: `addressOf`, `lastAreaHoldingTown`, `townsIn`), the town's own area by default; it refuses `TownNotFound`, then `AreaNotFound { area }` outside 1 to `lastArea` (the highest area holding a town plus one), and answers `RegionMap` with `terrainOf(area)` and sites 1 to `sitesPerArea`, each free or holding `{ name, isOwn }`. `Town` carries a `JobSlot` (`idle`, or an `out` `Job` with `site`, `team` (a `Hires`), `hours`, `sentAt`, `oneWaySeconds`, `pay`, `payPercent` and an optional `calledBackAt`, all fixed at dispatch); the team stays in `hires`, and `town.hiresAtHomeAt(at)` subtracts it. `sendJob({ playerId, site, team, hours }, { towns, map, news, catalog, clock })` resolves the town, then refuses in order `InvalidTeam`, `JobHoursOutOfRange { hours, maxHours }`, `TeamAlreadyOut`, `JobSiteOutOfBounds`, `JobToOwnSite`, `SiteHeld`, then `NotEnoughHiresAtHome`; it debits nothing, fixes the road with `jobOneWaySeconds` (the slowest kind's `roadPercent` and the season's `durationPercent.road`) and the pay with `jobPayOf` (`jobPayOfMilliseconds`: per paid resource of the site's terrain the lower of heads × rate × time × the season's `productionPercent` and the team's carry share; money never). `jobInstantsOf` and `jobPhaseAt` derive `outbound`, `working` and `returning` on read. `callJobBack({ playerId, sentAt }, deps)` refuses `JobNotFound { sentAt }`, then `JobAlreadyReturning`, sets `calledBackAt` and overwrites the pay: none while outbound, the worked time at the stored `payPercent` while working. `settleTownTo(town, catalog, at)`, shared by `resolvedTownOf` and `answerReview`, finishes the building work due by the return, returns the team (pay credited even above storage, `storedAt` the return, `jobBack` or `jobCalledBack`), then finishes the rest. `Town.restore` refuses an empty or fractional team (`InvalidTeam`), hours not whole from 1 (`InvalidJobHours`), a pay percent not whole from 1 (`InvalidPayPercent`), a call back outside the sent work (`SlotStartsAfterFinish`) and a return before `storedAt`. Tests pass `testing/inMemoryRegionMap.ts`.
- Clean-ups (#785, ADR 040, ADR 020): `wasteSizeOf(site, terms)` places waste on a free site when the high 24 bits of `mixed32` over `region`, `area` and `site` fall under `siteFraction × 2^24`, the low byte modulo 3 giving the size 1, 2 or 3 (`WasteSize`); nothing stores where waste is. `wasteAt(sizeTerms, lastCleanedAt, at)` is the size's `maxWaste` for a site never cleaned, else `min(maxWaste, floor(maxWaste × elapsed / regrowHours))` from 0. `TownSettings.waste` is `WasteTerms` (`siteFraction`, `moneyPerPoint`, `materialPerPoint`, `sizes`). A clean-up is a `Job` with `hours` 0 and a `cleanUp` (`JobCleanUp`: `size`, `waste`, `strength`, `hasArrived`), fixed at dispatch, so the job's road, slot, call back and return are shared; `CleanUpJob` is a job that holds one. `sendCleanUp({ playerId, site, team }, { towns, map, cleanUps, news, catalog, clock })` resolves the town, then refuses in order `InvalidTeam`, `TeamAlreadyOut`, `JobSiteOutOfBounds`, `JobToOwnSite`, `SiteHeld` (`admitJobSite`, shared with `sendJob`), `NotAWasteSite { area, site }` (no waste placed there), `NoWasteYet { area, site }` (its waste reads 0 since its last clean-up), then `NotEnoughHiresAtHome`; the waste is read at `now` through `CleanUpRepository.lastCleanedAt(site, at)`, the strength is `strengthOf(team)`, and `coversWaste` (strength at least the waste) decides the outcome: the pay is `cleanUpPayOf` (money and material per point, each capped at half the summed `carryOf`) or nothing, never scaled by the season (`payPercent` is `jobSeasonAt`'s `unchangedPayPercent`). A covering clean-up is recorded at dispatch (`CleanUpRepository.record`, `CleanUp { townId, sentAt, site, cleanedAt }`, `cleanedAt` the arrival), so the site reads 0 only from the arrival; `callJobBack` takes `cleanUps` and cancels the team's record (`cancel(townId, sentAt)`), so a team called back on the way out cleans nothing. `settleTownTo` applies the arrival first on the timeline (building work due by it first): `Town.arriveAtWaste` sets `hasArrived` and answers `cleanUpDone { site, reward }` or `cleanUpFailed { site }` at the arrival; the return then credits the pay with `jobBack`, as a job's. No hire is lost. `Town.restore` refuses a clean-up with hours (`InvalidJobHours`) and an unknown size, a waste not whole from 0, a strength not whole from 1 or an arrival after a call back (`InvalidCleanUp`). Tests pass `testing/inMemoryCleanUps.ts`.
